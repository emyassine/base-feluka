## Founder-led by design

Numerimondes is founder-led and works through **project-based assignments**. The founder provides direction and accountability, coordinating qualification, proposal development and delivery.

## Assemble expertise around each assignment

The delivery team is shaped by the assignment. Independent developers, consultants, operators and specialist companies can contribute alongside the founder when their expertise fits the work. Here, “team” means the people who act together on delivery; it does not imply a particular employment relationship.

## Agree on responsibilities

Responsibilities, reporting, confidentiality and deliverables are agreed before work begins. Numerimondes remains accountable to clients for the assignments it contracts to deliver.

# Innovation

## Curiosity with a direction.

Ideas can reveal new ways to work, new products to create and unmet needs worth exploring. At Numerimondes, research connects business insight and <span class="silky-i">technological inquiry</span> with the conditions required to put an idea to work.

Our areas of interest span enterprise operations, applications, infrastructure, applied AI, interoperability, publishing workflows and new service models.

## How we develop ideas

1. **Observe and investigate** — find recurring problems, emerging possibilities and market signals.
2. **Form a hypothesis** — describe who benefits, how and under what conditions.
3. **Examine feasibility** — evaluate technical, financial and operational constraints.
4. **Prototype or structure** — create a specification, demonstration, business model or project plan.
5. **Decide the next step** — develop further, partner, pilot, reposition or stop.

The process depends on available evidence and the maturity of each initiative.

# SPMPST

**Draft specification · In development**

## Portable content structures for site-building workflows.

SPMPST stands for **Standard Portable Markdown Pre-SSG Tree**. It is a specification initiative created at Numerimondes to define a <span class="silky-i">portable structure</span> for Markdown content, configuration and related resources before delivery to site-generation tools.

## Design goals

- Author content as understandable Markdown and structured resources.
- Keep content organization portable across rendering and build targets.
- Support multilingual sites, metadata, navigation and related assets.
- Support offline-capable built outputs, with dependencies controlled by the target implementation.
- Explore real-time rendering workflows, extensible components and automation-friendly conventions.
- Produce output and packaging per site.

## Current state

The specification is a draft. A reference preparation workflow and a VitePress-oriented adapter approach are part of the current technical work; a Feluqa/Rust adapter and broader cross-adapter conformance work remain planned. Individual capabilities should be validated against an implementation before being treated as supported.

## Collaboration

We welcome technical discussions around content portability, static site generation, documentation tooling and interoperability.
