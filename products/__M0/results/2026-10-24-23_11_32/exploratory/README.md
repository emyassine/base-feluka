# Exploratory measurements

These earlier measurements are retained for historical context only. They predate the final preregistered campaign and are **not inputs** to `../m0-score.json` or to the final conclusions in `../../DECISIONS_MO_RESULTS.md`.

- `build-metrics.json`: early build timing sample, superseded by `../final-build-metrics.json`.
- `memory-metrics.json`: early RSS sample, superseded by `../memory-repeat-suite.json`.
- `target-smoke.json`: early partial target check, superseded by `../target-build-suite.json`.
- `wasm-host-bench.json` and `wasm-host-suite.json`: early WASM samples, superseded by `../wasm-host-final-suite.json`.

The final score inputs are named explicitly in `../../scripts/score_m0.py` and hashed in `../m0-score.json`. The repository evidence manifest also records these historical files while making the distinction visible in the directory structure.
