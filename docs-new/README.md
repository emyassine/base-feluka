intent, current reality, and remaining work separately.

## Areas

- [`knowledge/`](knowledge/README.md): product intent, architecture decisions,
  durable context, and research notes.
- [`exists/`](exists/README.md): implemented capabilities, current contracts,
  and measured evidence. Status documents must also say what is missing.
- [`_todo/`](_todo/README.md): planned, incomplete, or unverified work.

The repository root [README](../README.md) remains the quick start. Historical
benchmark source and raw evidence stay under `products/__M0/`; do not rewrite
its measurements or hashes.
