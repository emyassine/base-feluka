# Deploy your app

The framework does not yet provide a qualified `feluqa deploy` flow. Deployment, backup, restore, secrets, TLS, migrations and monitoring need an operator-owned setup.

Feluqa's product direction includes hosted deployments and local servers that keep working without Internet. Do not treat the local-first requirement as proof that workstation packaging or air-gapped installation is already implemented.

See [Platform Support](../04.guides/03.platform-support.en.md) and [the roadmap](../999-roadmap/01.index.en.md).
