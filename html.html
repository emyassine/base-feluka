01 / The company

Independent.
Inquisitive.
Practical.

Numerimondes SARL AU is an independent consulting and solutions company based in Casablanca, Morocco.

We investigate commercial and operational challenges, examine opportunities, propose strategies, design technology, and coordinate the work required to make ideas useful in practice.

Our model is mission-based. The scope and team are shaped by the assignment, with specialist partners involved where their expertise is needed. Engagements are led directly, with clear responsibilities and written terms.

We also develop our own ventures and technical projects, applying research and engineering to problems we consider worth pursuing.

05 / How we work

Clarity before commitment.

We work best with business owners, executives, and leaders in operations, HR or IT who have a defined objective and the authority to move work forward.

Our first concern is understanding the issue, its consequences and the decisions it requires. A suitable engagement then receives a written scope and a delivery structure proportionate to the work.

What you can expect
  1. 01Direct communication with the person leading the engagement.
  2. 02A written scope, responsibilities and commercial terms before work starts.
  3. 03Specialists and partners brought in when the assignment calls for them.
  4. 04A candid assessment of feasibility, constraints and whether we are a suitable fit.
