# Documentation migration ledger

Migration date: 2026-10-28. Scope: all 57 files from `docs-new/`, the three
architecture/product/compilation README files and `_ToRead_later.en.md`: 61 sources.
Every source body is retained, including the empty CLI note. Only Markdown link
destinations are relocated or repaired inside retained bodies. New notices,
current baseline, roadmap summaries and decision records surround that evidence.

The old docs tree and four root note files have been moved, not left as competing
sources of truth. The repository README, AGENTS.md and dependency-policy consumer
link point to `docs/`. Legal notices, crate-local reviews, product READMEs and
raw `products/__M0/` evidence remain owned in place. Nothing was published.

## Verification

`migration-manifest.json` records original byte counts/SHA-256 and retained-body
SHA-256. The migration check validates every body between provenance markers.
Local links are checked independently; external URLs and runtime acceptance are
not revalidated here. The full original source can also be recovered from Git.

```sh
python3 scripts/check-docs.py --migration
```

Accepted decisions are recorded in their product, architecture, developer and organization chapters. Unresolved proposals remain in the [open-question register](../07.research-and-notes/12.open-questions.en.md) until the owner decides them. Historical decision records are archived under research and notes; there is no permanent `docs/decisions/` section. New documentation follows [the house style](../../README_Voice_And_Tone.en.md) and [its writing references](../DOCS_GUIDELINES.md).
The original-root monorepo and product proposals are consolidated into their
architecture/intent chapters in full; the shorter previous intent and historical
navigation remain separate, clearly labeled, to avoid silently deleting details.
Future editorial consolidation must retain that provenance.
