# Operational software, built around how you actually work

Feluqa is a platform for building the application your organization needs, then running it wherever your people and machines are:

- The browser,
- A workstation,
- A phone,
- An edge device,
- A server.

NUMERIMONDES designs and builds it with you. Or your own engineers build it. Either way, you use what you build, and that right is guaranteed.

[See what is tested today](/products/)

## The problem we keep hearing

Most organizations run on a patchwork.

A spreadsheet that one person understands. A packaged tool that fits 70 percent of the process and fights you on the rest. A separate app for the office, another for the shop floor, a third for people in the field, each with its own login, its own data, and its own vendor.

When something changes, a regulation, a new site, a new kind of device, you do not change one system. You renegotiate several.

## What you get

### One application, every place it runs

You describe the workflow once. Feluqa builds it for each target you operate, with the interface suited to the device and a runtime suited to the environment. Same logic, same data model, same rules, everywhere.

### A product you are allowed to use

Creating applications with the Feluqa SDK is free of charge, for personal and commercial work. The products you build are yours to use, for yourself, your organization, or your client. The Feluqa Platform itself is proprietary, and the written proposal states exactly what that means for you before any work starts.

### Software you can inspect

Applications are assembled from standardized structs and components with clear contracts, not from one-off glue. That makes a system easier to review, easier to hand over, and easier for an AI agent to read and propose changes to, with a person approving each change.

### Honest status on every page

Each capability is marked Implemented, Partial, Target, or Exploring.

You will never have to guess.

## Who it is for

### Defence, security, and field operations

**The situation.** Units and agencies need software that fits their procedures, runs on the hardware they actually have, and stays under their control.

**What we are designing for.** Ground-side drone telemetry, sensor-fusion ingestion, secure logistics, and workstation tools for operators.

**Status.** Target. These are the workloads the platform is shaped around. Please talk to us before assuming any specific certification, accreditation, or deployment mode: we will tell you plainly what exists and what would have to be built.

### Small and medium businesses

**The situation.** You are too specific for a packaged tool and too small for a software department.

**What we build.** The system around your actual process: orders, stock, quotes, scheduling, customer follow-up, internal reporting. One application that works on the office computer and on a phone in the workshop.

**What it costs.** Terms are agreed per engagement, in writing, before work begins. The SDK is free; development, support, and maintenance may be charged.

### Very small businesses

**The situation.** A few people, a lot of manual steps, no budget for a long project.

**What we build.** A narrow tool that replaces the spreadsheet and the paper, delivered in a scope you can afford, that can grow without being rewritten.

### Factories and industrial sites

**The situation.** Machines, sensors, operators, and a back office that all need the same picture of what is happening.

**What we build.** Real-time dashboards, traceability, maintenance and shift tools, and the screens operators use on the floor, connected to the systems you already run.

**Status.** Partial and Target. Selected platform foundations exist today. Complete runtime shells and dynamic module installation are not finished.

### Larger organizations and public bodies

**The situation.** Several departments, several vendors, and a growing list of tools nobody fully owns.

**What we build.** A shared foundation for the applications you build over the next years, with common identity, access control, and audit, so each new application starts from what the last one already solved.

## How an engagement works

1. **A conversation.** You tell us about the users, the workflows, the environment, and what a good result looks like.
2. **A written proposal.** Scope, deliverables, who owns customer-specific work, Feluqa usage rights, deployment, support, maintenance, and fees. All of it, before any work begins.
3. **A working prototype of the key flow.** You see your own process running before the full build, and you can say "not like that" while it is still cheap to change.
4. **The build.** Developed against the agreed scope, reviewed against the security and architecture guidelines, with progress shared as it happens.
5. **Deployment and support.** We deploy it, document it, and stay available. Support and maintenance terms are those in your proposal.

## Questions you should ask us

### Is it ready for production?

Parts of the platform are implemented, and we mark which. A few products are tested, listed on the [Products](/products/) page. For anything beyond that, we will agree a scope that states what exists and what must be built, and we will not sell you the second as if it were the first.

### Who owns what we build?

You may use the products you create, for yourself, your organization, or your client, and that right is guaranteed. Rights in customer-specific source code are defined in your proposal. Feluqa's SDK and Platform remain proprietary software owned by NUMERIMONDES SARL AU.

### Are we locked in?

The Platform is proprietary, so you depend on it the way you would depend on any platform vendor. What is guaranteed is your right to use what you build. Some components will be released under open-source licences over time; each release will say which components and under which licence. Ask us about exit terms and we will put the answer in the proposal.

### Can it run in a restricted or disconnected environment?

That is a design goal and part of what the runtime profiles are for. It is not something to assume. Tell us your constraints and we will answer for your case.

### Why not buy a packaged product?

If a packaged product fits, buy it. We are for the cases where it does not: your process is specific, you run on more than one kind of device, or you need to control the software and the data.

### Why not build everything from scratch?

You can, and many do. The cost is rebuilding identity, access control, audit, configuration, data access, and deployment for every project. Feluqa provides those foundations so your effort goes into your workflows.

### What does it cost?

The SDK is free for creating applications. Development, licensing, support, and maintenance fees, where they apply, are agreed per engagement and written down before work begins.
