## Before publishing

- Implement functional contact form or verified contact details.
- Check that routes and headings fit the target site builder.
- Treat all conceptual showcases as concepts until demonstrable.
- Review commission, eligibility, data privacy, and recruitment statements against the final agreement and legal requirements.
- Do not render HTML comments (internal authoring notes) on the site.
- Do not imply Feluqa, SPMPST or The Best Recruit are production services.
- Confirm founder biography and any outward-facing professional claims.

This pack deliberately does not copy confidential partner names or personal details from proposals into public web copy.
