# Consulting & business development

## Better questions. Clearer decisions. Actionable plans.

We help organizations investigate operating challenges, explore new opportunities and structure initiatives with commercial and operational reality in mind.

### Areas of work

Business-development research, operational diagnosis, strategy workshops, process mapping, project feasibility, options analysis, requirements and implementation planning.

### What an engagement may produce

An opportunity assessment, decision brief, process map, prioritized roadmap, business case, technical brief or project proposal. The exact deliverables are agreed before work starts.

### How it helps

Make choices with a clearer view of expected benefits, cost, dependencies and risks. Discover where a targeted improvement or a larger initiative is justified.

### Approach

We begin with a qualification discussion. Where substantial investigation is required, we propose a defined discovery assignment with documented scope and pricing.
