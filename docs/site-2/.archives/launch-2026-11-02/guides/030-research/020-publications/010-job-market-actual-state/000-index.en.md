# Job market: actual state

There is no single “job market”. There are **overlapping markets** — by geography, by skill stack, by institutional gatekeeping — that only look unified in headlines.

## What to watch

1. **[[skill-obsolescence|Skill obsolescence]]** — half-life of mid-stack skills under automation and platform churn.
2. **[[labour-market-signals|Labour-market signals]]** — vacancies, wage dispersion, and time-to-hire as noisy but useful instruments.
3. **Institutional lag** — schools, procurement, and HR systems that still hire for yesterday’s job descriptions.

## Working claim

Operators who treat hiring as a **capability portfolio** (what we can train, what we buy, what we partner for) outperform those who treat it as a perpetual vacancy hunt.
