# How to contribute

Several paths feed the live community models:

1. **Forum** — answer questions and open threads in [General](/community/forum/general), [Webkernel](/community/forum/webkernel), [Education](/community/forum/education), or [Jobs](/community/forum/jobs).
2. **Events** — attend sessions and earn attestations via [upcoming events](/community/events/upcoming).
3. **Missions** — apply to published [freelance jobs](/community/freelance-jobs).
4. **Consulting** — join the expert network via [become a consultant](/community/become-consultant).





Recognition of outstanding contributors lives on the [hall of fame](/community/contributors/hall-of-fame).
