# <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Capabilities</span>

Our capabilities are for SMEs, owner-managed businesses, professional practices and operating teams seeking practical business or technology improvements. We work across consumer and commerce, financial and professional services, healthcare and learning, industrials and logistics, and technology, media and telecom.

Capabilities describe the work Numerimondes can investigate, design or coordinate. They explain useful methods, questions and possible outcomes. They are an editorial guide to our areas of expertise, not a list of guaranteed deliverables or a promise that every specialty is permanently staffed in-house.

The right mix of Numerimondes and specialist partner expertise depends on each mission. A capability may be useful even when there is no matching service currently available.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Explore by area</span>

- [Business Performance](/capabilities/business-performance) — understand operations, commercial workflows and improvement opportunities.
- [Software and Automation](/capabilities/software-automation) — shape applications, integrations and workflow automation around real work.
- [Infrastructure and Connected Operations](/capabilities/infrastructure-connected-operations) — plan dependable systems for teams, equipment and field environments.
- [People and Organizations](/capabilities/people-organizations) — examine coordination, learning and workforce needs.
- [Research and Ventures](/capabilities/research-ventures) — explore new concepts, technologies and projects.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Capabilities</span>, services and ventures

Capabilities explain what we know how to explore and how we approach a problem. The [Services Marketplace](/services/marketplace) is a demonstration catalogue; published pricing pages define current commercial terms. Company projects such as [Feluqa](/company/projects/feluqa) and [The Best Recruit, our remote recruitment service](/company/projects/the-best-recruit) show their status on each page.

An article may link to a related service or project where one exists. This keeps the knowledge library useful without turning every topic into a separate commercial offer.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Browse from different angles</span>

These capabilities can also be considered by business challenge, industry or technology. Those classifications will grow as the library develops; this initial collection groups articles by their main area of practice.
