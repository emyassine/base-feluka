# Research and Ventures

Research and venture development help examine possibilities before an organization commits to a full implementation. The work can begin with a question about a market, an emerging technology, a business model or an unmet operational need.

## Explore with a decision in mind

A focused exploration defines the question, available evidence and constraints. It may include desk research, stakeholder discovery, comparison of approaches, a feasibility review or a small prototype. The output should make the next decision clearer, including when the evidence suggests stopping or changing direction.

Prototypes and concepts are experiments. They do not establish product readiness, validated demand or a commitment to launch. Claims should reflect what was actually tested and learned.
