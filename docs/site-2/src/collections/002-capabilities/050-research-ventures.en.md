# <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Research and Ventures</span>

For founders, business owners and product leaders, research and venture development examine possibilities before the organization commits to a full implementation. The work can begin with a question about a market, an emerging technology, a business model or an unmet operational need.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Internal standards</span>

Internal standards document agreed practices, requirements or technical conventions so teams can apply them consistently. Research can clarify where standards are needed, what evidence supports them and how they should be reviewed as work changes.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Experimental platforms</span>

Experimental platforms provide a bounded way to explore a technology, workflow or service concept. A focused exploration defines the question, evidence and constraints; it may include desk research, stakeholder discovery, comparison of approaches, a feasibility review or a small prototype. Prototypes are experiments and do not establish product readiness.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>New business concepts</span>

New business concepts can be examined through customer and market research, operating assumptions, feasibility and small-scale tests. The output should make the next decision clearer, including when evidence suggests stopping or changing direction; it does not establish validated demand or a commitment to launch.
