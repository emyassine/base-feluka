# <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Research and Ventures</span>

For founders, business owners and product leaders, research and venture development examine possibilities before the organization commits to a full implementation. The work can begin with a question about a market, an emerging technology, a business model or an unmet operational need.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Explore with a decision in mind</span>

A focused exploration defines the question, available evidence and constraints. It may include desk research, stakeholder discovery, comparison of approaches, a feasibility review or a small prototype. The output should make the next decision clearer, including when the evidence suggests stopping or changing direction.

Prototypes and concepts are experiments. They do not establish product readiness, validated demand or a commitment to launch. Claims should reflect what was actually tested and learned.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Projects and evolving ideas</span>

Numerimondes projects can connect research to practical development. [Feluqa](/company/projects/feluqa) is an application platform in development. Other initiatives may be shared as their scope and status become clear. A project page describes an initiative; it does not make that initiative a purchasable service.
