# <span class='silky-bbi+115'>People and Organizations</span>

This capability is for business owners, HR leaders and operations managers working to improve coordination, learning and workforce processes. Organizational performance depends on how responsibilities, information and decisions come together.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Understand the people side of work</span>

An assignment may explore role clarity, handoffs, reporting, onboarding, learning needs or the way teams use their tools. Interviews, workflow reviews and feedback from the people doing the work can reveal where procedures and daily practice differ.

Possible interventions include clearer operating guidance, training resources, revised coordination routines or changes to supporting systems. These options should fit the organization’s capacity and be reviewed with the people affected.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Workforce and recruitment needs</span>

For business owners, HR leaders and managers, workforce planning can clarify skills, capacity and role requirements. Recruitment requires clear role criteria, candidate consent and agreed service commitments.
