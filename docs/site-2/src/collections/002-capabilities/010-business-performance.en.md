# <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Business Performance</span>

For business owners, executives and operations leaders, business performance work makes visible where time, information or value is lost, so they can decide what to change.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Operational diagnostics</span>

An operational diagnostic examines how work moves across people, processes, tools and decisions. It can combine stakeholder interviews, process mapping, document review, available performance data and observation of day-to-day work.

The investigation asks practical questions: Where do requests wait? Which handoffs cause rework? What information is missing when a decision is made? Which measures reflect the outcome the organization actually wants?

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>From findings to action</span>

Findings are organized by impact, evidence and effort. Depending on scope, a useful output may be a current-state map, a prioritized set of improvement options, a measurement plan or a roadmap for further work. Results depend on access to relevant people and data; an assessment cannot guarantee a specific financial or operational gain.

This capability can inform an [operational assessment or process improvement mission](/services/marketplace). The marketplace currently presents illustrative catalogue data; availability and scope must be confirmed for each request.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Related areas</span>

Commercial workflows and strategic planning often connect with [operations and business advisory](/services/marketplace). Software and reporting tools may support an improvement, but should follow a clear understanding of the work itself.
