# <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Infrastructure and Connected Operations</span>

This capability is for organizations that depend on reliable systems, work across locations or need clearer control of their data and recovery responsibilities. The right design depends on workload, connectivity, recovery needs, technical ownership and the conditions in which people operate.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Plan for the operating context</span>

An infrastructure review can map current services, dependencies, access, backup arrangements and support responsibilities. For teams working beyond a central office, the assessment may also consider intermittent connectivity, mobile data collection, equipment and how information is synchronized.

Self-hosting can offer control, but it also brings responsibility for updates, security, backups, monitoring and recovery. A design should make those responsibilities explicit and testable.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>From design to operation</span>

Depending on the assignment, work may include an architecture or migration plan, deployment support, monitoring requirements, operating procedures or a scoped technical review. Security assurance requires an appropriate specialist scope and evidence; a general infrastructure discussion is not a certification or security audit.
