# Account access and workspace use policy

<img src="/images/policies/account-access-workspace.webp" alt="One of several separated stone forms sits in a narrow pool of warm light.">

## Purpose and scope

This policy applies to the Numerimondes account area, including client project spaces and team access. It sets expectations for people who create, use or administer an account. A link or account does not itself create a client engagement, guarantee that a service is available, or change the terms of a signed agreement.

## Authorized accounts and access

Use an account only for the client, team or collaboration purpose for which access was granted. Provide accurate registration details and keep account contact information current. Do not create an account in another person’s name, impersonate another user, share credentials, or let an unauthorized person use an authenticated session.

Client organizations are responsible for requesting access only for people authorized to act for them and for notifying Numerimondes when a user no longer needs that access. Team and collaborator access is restricted to the work and permissions assigned to that person. Access to another client’s workspace or records is prohibited unless an explicit written agreement authorizes it.

## Account holder responsibilities

Account holders must protect their sign-in credentials and devices, use only the account features they are authorized to use, and promptly report suspected account compromise or an access error through the [contact page](/company/contact), marking the message **Security concern**. Do not include passwords, access tokens or unnecessary personal or confidential records in a report.

Use the account area for its stated project, billing, deliverable, support or team purposes. Do not upload information you are not authorized to provide, attempt to bypass access controls, disrupt the service, or use an account to violate another person’s rights or applicable law.

## Roles, changes and suspension

Access should be limited to the people and permissions needed for the assigned work. Administrators and account holders must review access when responsibilities change and request removal of access that is no longer needed. Numerimondes may restrict or suspend access to investigate a suspected compromise, protect people or records, respond to a legal requirement, or address a material breach of these rules. Where appropriate, Numerimondes will provide notice and a route to resolve the issue.

Closing or suspending an account does not automatically erase records that must be retained for an active engagement, accounting, security or legal purpose. Requests about account data are handled under the [account and CRM privacy notice](/legal/account-and-crm-privacy) and applicable agreements.

## Service terms and questions

Project scope, fees, invoicing, support, deliverables and account-specific responsibilities are defined by the applicable written proposal or agreement. This policy does not promise a particular availability level, response time, retention period or feature. For a security issue, follow the [security reporting policy](/policies/security-reporting). For other account questions, use the [contact page](/company/contact).
