# Prospect research and outreach policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy governs how Numerimondes staff, contractors and partners research businesses and professional contacts for Numerimondes or an agreed partner engagement. It applies to public websites, search and map listings, professional and social platforms, discussion communities, messaging groups and manually conducted research. The [prospecting and business contact privacy notice](/legal/prospecting-and-business-contact-privacy) explains how the resulting information is handled.

## Before research begins

### Define the task

Record who requested the work, the business purpose, target organizations or audience, countries, permitted sources, intended contact channels, data fields, recipients, retention criteria and the party responsible for each decision. For partner work, record whether Numerimondes acts only on documented instructions or independently determines any purpose or method.

### Check formalities and channel rules

Before collecting personal data, confirm the applicable privacy-law basis, required CNDP declaration or authorization, and any cross-border transfer formalities. Before outreach, check the law for the recipient’s country and the specific channel. Moroccan Law 09-08 has specific restrictions on direct marketing by email, automated calls and similar means. A permission to collect or view information does not itself authorize a marketing message.

### Separate recruitment

Recruitment and candidate evaluation use a distinct process, access group and purpose from sales prospecting. Do not transfer candidate information into a commercial lead list. Confirm CNDP requirements for the relevant HR processing before collection or screening.

## Source and method limits

### Publicly available sources

Use only information relevant to the documented business purpose. Record the original source and date. Follow applicable site terms, platform rules, privacy settings, group rules, technical access controls and rate limits. Public visibility is not a blanket license to collect, reuse, disclose or contact.

### Automated and manual collection

Automated extraction may be used only when applicable law, source terms and technical controls permit it. When those conditions do not permit automation, do not automate. Manual research is also subject to privacy, confidentiality, source and platform rules; it must not be used to evade a prohibition on collection or reuse.

Do not bypass logins, access controls, CAPTCHAs, rate limits, group membership restrictions or privacy settings. Do not use fake accounts, borrowed credentials, deceptive personas or fabricated explanations to obtain information.

### Messaging communities

Use information from WhatsApp groups, channels and similar communities only when access is legitimate and the community’s stated rules permit the intended research. Do not collect member lists, private messages or personal contact details merely because they are technically visible. Do not add members to a CRM or contact them for marketing without checking the applicable legal basis, channel rules and required disclosures.

## Data minimization and quality

### Permitted business details

Collect organization details and professional contact information only to the degree needed for the defined task. Avoid sensitive personal data, private-life details, credentials, family information and unrelated personal observations. Do not create speculative profiles or infer sensitive characteristics.

### Verify and correct

Mark uncertain information as unverified. Check accuracy before acting on it. Correct or remove information that is inaccurate, irrelevant or no longer necessary. Keep a minimal suppression entry when needed to respect a no-contact request.

## Honest outreach

### Identify the caller or researcher

Use the person’s real name and accurately state their role, affiliation and the organization they represent. Explain the genuine reason for contact when required, answer reasonable questions about the source of the information, and provide a usable privacy contact and opt-out route.

Do not describe a person as an independent economic-intelligence consultant, client, auditor, regulator, journalist or security assessor unless that description is accurate and the role is actually authorized. Do not conceal commercial outreach behind a purported research task. The work being unpaid does not make a misleading role or unauthorized activity acceptable.

### No unauthorized security assessment

Passive review of lawfully accessible public business information is not permission to test an organization’s systems or site. Do not scan, probe, attempt access, test vulnerabilities, solicit confidential information or enter facilities without explicit written authorization from someone authorized to grant it. An organization that has not commissioned or authorized an assessment is not a test target. No report should imply that Numerimondes performed a technical or physical assessment if it did not.

## Partner handling

### Written scope and responsibilities

Before work starts, agree in writing the partner’s identity, purpose, instructions, permitted sources and methods, communication responsibility, data recipients, security, retention, deletion or return, and how rights requests and objections will be handled. Use a data-processing agreement when the relationship legally requires one. A partner’s use for its own purpose must not be treated as covered merely by a Numerimondes notice.

### Disclosure and no-contact requests

Do not give a partner identifiable contact data unless the documented arrangement and applicable law permit that disclosure and any required information or consent has been provided. Record objections and suppress the contact for the relevant marketing use. Do not pass an opted-out contact to a partner for the same purpose.

## Records, retention and incident handling

### Research records

Keep a proportionate record of the task, source, date, purpose, data categories, recipient and outreach outcome. Restrict access to people assigned to the task. Store the record only in approved systems and do not place it in public documents or personal accounts.

### Retention and deletion

Set a retention or review date for each list and project. Delete or return partner data at the end of the agreed period or engagement, unless a specific legal or security obligation requires retention. Retain only the minimum suppression information needed to avoid future unwanted contact.

### Complaints and incidents

Forward access, correction, deletion and objection requests promptly to the person responsible for privacy requests. Report suspected unauthorized collection, disclosure, account access or data loss under the [security reporting policy](/policies/security-reporting). Pause the affected collection or outreach until the issue is reviewed.

## Review

The task owner reviews these controls whenever the target country, source, platform, data type, partner role or outreach channel changes. New campaigns and partner research must not start until their privacy, source-access and channel requirements have been checked.
