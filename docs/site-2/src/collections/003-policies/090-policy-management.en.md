# Policy management policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets the working rules for creating and maintaining policies and legal notices published by Numerimondes. It applies to documents that describe commitments, boundaries, eligibility, responsibilities, rights or operating rules for clients, partners, visitors and the Numerimondes team.

For document ownership, review, approval, publication, versioning and withdrawal, this policy prevails over conflicting administrative wording elsewhere. It does not replace subject-specific terms, a signed agreement or applicable law, and it does not change the rights or obligations stated in a legal notice or agreement.

A policy is a public statement of rules or commitments for a defined subject. A legal notice or set of terms states the information or obligations that apply to its own subject. Guides explain how to use a feature; they do not create a new commitment unless a policy or agreement says so.

## Ownership and drafting

Each policy must have a named accountable owner, a clear audience and scope, and a stated effective or review date before publication. The owner checks that the text is accurate, actionable, consistent with related policies and guides, and understandable to its intended audience. The owner identifies operational dependencies and any required legal, security, privacy or commercial review.

Drafts must be marked as drafts and must not be presented as current commitments. Where a policy depends on a contract, law or service-specific terms, it must explain that relationship without suggesting that the policy replaces them.

## Review and approval

The accountable owner reviews the policy with the people responsible for the affected service or process. Material privacy, security, legal, financial or contractual commitments require review by the relevant responsible person before publication. The person authorized to make the commitment approves the final text.

Reviewers verify scope, definitions, responsibilities, exceptions, dates, links and consistency with related documents. If a required review or approval has not happened, the policy remains a draft.

## Publication and records

Published policies must be accessible from the appropriate policy collection and include a clear title, summary, current status and effective or last-updated date. The canonical page is the current version. The owner keeps a record of material changes, approval and superseded versions so the reason and timing of a change can be traced.

## Changes and withdrawal

The owner reviews a policy when its service, process, legal context or supporting technology materially changes, and at least once every 12 months. A material change requires the same relevant review and approval as a new policy. The page must summarize material changes and show when they take effect.

When a policy is replaced or retired, the owner updates links and marks the former page as superseded or withdrawn, with a link to the replacement where one exists. Do not silently change a material commitment while leaving an old effective date in place.

## Exceptions and interpretation

An exception must be authorized by the person accountable for the policy and recorded with its scope and duration. Exceptions cannot override applicable law or a signed agreement. Questions about a policy should be directed to the contact or service owner identified in that policy.
