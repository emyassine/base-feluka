# Service availability and support policy

Availability and support depend on the service, hosting arrangement, operating responsibilities and agreed support plan. A proposal or service agreement should state the supported system, support channel, service hours, response expectations, maintenance approach, backup responsibilities and recovery assumptions where they apply.

## What we do not promise by default

We do not promise 24/7 support, a universal response time, continuous availability, a recovery time or recovery point, or uninterrupted access unless the relevant written terms explicitly state it. A website or service description alone does not establish an uptime commitment.

Hosting, domain renewal, monitoring, backups, updates, incident response and ongoing maintenance are separate responsibilities unless the agreement assigns them. Clients operating their own systems remain responsible for the tasks assigned to them, including access, backups and recovery.

When a service needs firm availability or support commitments, those requirements must be assessed and agreed before launch. See the [Client engagement policy](/policies/client-engagement) for how project scope and responsibilities are documented.
