# Partner and contractor policy

Numerimondes works with independent firms and specialists when their role fits a defined opportunity. A conversation, introduction or inclusion in a network does not itself create a partnership, employment relationship, exclusivity, authority to represent Numerimondes or a right to payment.

## Written terms for each arrangement

Before work or a commission-eligible sale begins, the relevant parties agree in writing who contracts with the client, who owns the client relationship, what each party delivers, how acceptance and changes work, and how price, fees, invoices, expenses and payment timing are handled. Referral or commission eligibility, calculation, payer and payment trigger must be explicit.

A partner delivering work remains responsible for its own business and professional obligations. Partners may not promise scope, pricing, availability, security or other commitments on Numerimondes' behalf unless authorized in writing.

## What we do not publish or assume

We do not offer a universal commission percentage, guaranteed flow of work, automatic referral fee, exclusivity or standard payment schedule. Terms for one partner, country, product line or opportunity do not automatically apply to another. The applicable written agreement and deal-specific schedule define the arrangement.
