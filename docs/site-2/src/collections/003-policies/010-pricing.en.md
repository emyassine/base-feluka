# Services and pricing

<img src="/images/policies/pricing-scoped-value.webp" alt="An amber prism and stacked ceramic pieces balance on a dark slate beam.">

Numerimondes works with employers, SMEs, owner-managed businesses, professional practices and operating teams. We mobilize a team to fit each assignment, bringing together the relevant consulting, engineering and specialist skills as the scope requires.

We price the agreed work, not an assumed standard package. Each proposal explains the client need, intended outcome, scope, deliverables, pricing basis, responsibilities and payment terms. Capabilities describe areas we can investigate; service offers describe work that can be scoped for a client.

### <span class='silky-bbi'>Consumer and commerce</span> {#consumer}

Retailers, wholesalers, e-commerce businesses, local services and growing consumer brands. Relevant needs may include websites and catalogues, customer inquiries, stock and order workflows, sales reporting and operational improvements.

### <span class='silky-bbi'>Financial and professional services</span> {#financial}

Accounting and advisory firms, consultancies, legal practices and financial service providers. Relevant needs may include secure document workflows, client portals, reporting, recruitment and software that supports professional operations.

### <span class='silky-bbi'>Healthcare and learning</span> {#health}

Clinics, healthcare organizations, training centres, schools and fitness businesses. Relevant needs may include appointment journeys, training, team coordination, business software and information workflows.

### <span class='silky-bbi'>Industrials and logistics</span> {#industrials}

Manufacturers, distributors, importers, field-service teams and logistics operators. Relevant needs may include stock and order processes, field applications, integrations, infrastructure, continuity and operational visibility.

### <span class='silky-bbi'>Technology, media and telecom</span> {#technology}

Software companies, digital agencies, media businesses, telecom providers and technology-led SMEs. Relevant needs may include custom software, automation, systems integration, customer operations, recruitment and digital growth.

We confirm fit and scope with each client. Regulated, safety-critical or specialist work is limited to the agreed expertise, evidence and professional responsibilities.

### <span class='silky-bbi'>Remote recruitment: The Best Recruit</span> {#recruitment}

The Best Recruit is a remote recruitment service for employers, with candidate qualification and relevant information for hiring decisions. It is designed for HR teams, business owners and hiring managers filling defined roles, wherever the employer is located. The role, search, candidate information, remote interactions and service commitments are agreed for each mandate.

A fee rate of **46% of the candidate’s minimum monthly salary is planned**. Market research to validate the rate is planned soon; final pricing will be confirmed after the research. This planned rate is not yet a confirmed tariff.

### <span class='silky-bbi'>Business advisory</span> {#advisory}

Operational diagnostics, project discovery, process improvement and decision support for business owners and leaders who need to understand friction, compare options or prioritize investment. Each assessment or project defines its evidence, deliverables, decision points and acceptance.

### <span class='silky-bbi'>Software development and automation</span> {#software}

Custom business software, internal applications, dashboards, integrations, reporting and workflow automation for organizations whose spreadsheets, disconnected systems or manual processes no longer support the work. Requirements, user roles, data, integrations, security, deployment, ownership and support determine scope and milestones.

### <span class='silky-bbi'>Websites and search visibility</span> {#websites}

Business websites designed around discoverability, useful content and customer action. SEO is included. Professional practices, tradespeople and SMEs can use showcase websites; retailers and manufacturers can present products; training, fitness and local services can take bookings; other businesses can connect inquiries to a management or sales system.

**Website development starts at 25,000 Dhs HT, including static websites; SEO is included.** Pages, languages, integrations, content and search requirements are agreed in the proposal. See the [Website Development Policy](/policies/website-development-policy) for the minimum and scope boundaries.

### <span class='silky-bbi'>Infrastructure and security</span> {#infrastructure}

Infrastructure planning, deployment, monitoring, managed operations, continuity and scoped security reviews for organizations that depend on reliable systems, work across locations or need clearer responsibility for hosting and recovery. Systems, access, service hours, security scope, backups, response expectations and operating responsibilities are defined before delivery.

### <span class='silky-bbi'>People, training and customer operations</span> {#people}

Team training, process workshops, contact-centre setup and customer operations support for managers, HR leaders and service teams improving coordination, capability or customer contact. The participants, channels, languages, duration, preparation, reporting and ongoing capacity are agreed for the assignment.

### <span class='silky-bbi'>Research and new ventures</span> {#research}

Market exploration, feasibility, concept validation and prototyping for founders and business leaders deciding whether and how to develop a new offer, product or operating model. A separate project brief defines the question, evidence, prototype and next decision.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Websites: an asset that keeps working</span>

A shop may close for the night while its website remains available. Visitors can find information, explore products and send inquiries outside office hours. With the right content and integrations, a website can support sales and operations around the clock; the outcomes depend on the business, implementation and ongoing work.

Our website work uses digital tools to strengthen a business’s online presence and support its goals. It brings together four considerations:

| Consideration | What it means for the client |
| :--- | :--- |
| **A continuous operating channel** | A website can present information and receive inquiries at any hour. It can be connected to booking, CRM or management tools when included in scope. |
| **Long-term organic visibility** | SEO helps build discoverability through useful content and sound technical foundations, reducing reliance on paid advertising alone. |
| **Strategy, evidence and creative work** | Structure, content and design are chosen around the audience, customer journey and measurable business goals. |
| **Visibility as markets change** | Search engines and AI assistants are changing how customers find businesses. SEO and emerging generative-engine optimization (GEO) needs are assessed according to the market and the project. |

SEO is included in the website offer. Work to improve visibility in AI-generated answers (GEO) may require additional research, content or technical work; it is included only when stated in the project scope. Search competition, content, technical requirements, languages and integrations affect the work, which is why the stated starting price is a minimum rather than a universal project price. We mobilize the strategy, design, web development and SEO expertise each site requires.

| Website type | A good fit for |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Showcase website** | A professional practice, tradesperson or SME that wants to be found and present a credible offer. |
| **Catalogue website** | A retailer or manufacturer presenting products and receiving quotation requests. |
| **Website with appointments** | A training centre, fitness business or local service taking bookings. |
| **Website connected to a management system** | A business that wants each inquiry routed into its sales or operating workflow. |

A website is a business asset: the organization owns it, can measure how it performs and can improve it over time. Ownership, access, hosting, content and ongoing support are specified in the project terms.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>How we set a price</span>

We begin with the client outcome, current situation and constraints. We choose a pricing model that fits the work and the information available. A fixed scope can suit stable requirements; a formula can suit a small number of agreed inputs; ongoing work can use a periodic review when costs or capacity change. More complex pricing requires reliable data, clear approval rights and an agreed update process.

| Pricing model | Used when | Confirmed in writing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fixed-scope project** | Deliverables and acceptance conditions can be defined in advance. | Scope, assumptions, exclusions, price, milestones and change process. |
| **Discovery or feasibility** | The client needs evidence or options before deciding on implementation. | Research question, outputs, limits and any credit toward later work. |
| **Milestone-based delivery** | Work can progress through approved phases and decision points. | Deliverables, acceptance and payment for each phase. |
| **Time-based specialist engagement** | The work needs defined specialist capacity over an agreed period. | Rate basis, capacity limits, reporting and period. |
| **Recurring operations and support** | Responsibilities continue after an initial project. | Service window, capacity, response commitments, exclusions and review terms. |

Pricing should reflect the client need, relevant market conditions and the value the agreed work is designed to create. We do not guarantee a financial or operational result. The 46% recruitment fee rate remains planned until the upcoming market research is complete and the final terms are confirmed.
