# Client data handling policy

This policy describes the boundaries we use when a client shares information for an engagement. It does not replace the [website privacy notice](/legal/site-privacy) or a data-processing agreement where one is required.

## Use and access

We ask for information relevant to the agreed work and use it to deliver, support or account for that work. Before sensitive, personal or regulated information is shared, the parties must agree what is needed, who may access it, where it will be handled, and any required safeguards or instructions.

We do not use client information for unrelated purposes, publish it as a case study, or disclose it to another party without an agreed basis or authorization. Access should be limited to people who need the information for the engagement.

## Storage and lifecycle

The engagement terms should identify approved tools or hosting, client and provider responsibilities, transfer methods, retention or return expectations, and any deletion process. Where a project needs a specific location, residency, retention period or deletion evidence, that requirement must be agreed before the information is transferred.

We do not ask clients to send passwords, secret keys or other credentials through public contact forms or ordinary email. Use an agreed secure method for access information.

## Limits

Do not send information that is not needed for the work. Do not share information you are not authorized to provide. We do not claim a universal retention period, hosting location or certification across all projects; these depend on the systems and terms agreed for the engagement.
