# Client and CRM data handling policy

This policy describes the boundaries Numerimondes applies when information is submitted through an account or shared for an engagement. It does not replace the [website privacy notice](/legal/site-privacy), the [account and CRM privacy notice](/legal/account-and-crm-privacy), or a data-processing agreement where one is required.

## Account and CRM records

Account registration, sign-in, access administration, client workspace use and team collaboration can involve personal information and records about a client organization. Numerimondes must define the purpose and authorized users for each account workflow, limit access to the people who need it, and keep client workspaces separated according to the applicable account and engagement arrangements.

Account, authentication, permission-change and security records must be handled only for account operation, service delivery, security, accountability or another stated purpose. Access to security and audit records must be restricted to people who need them for those purposes. They must not be repurposed for unrelated marketing or behavioral tracking.

Before a CRM workflow is enabled, its data fields, purpose, responsible party, service providers, access model, retention criteria, deletion or return process and any cross-border handling must be documented. Where Numerimondes processes information on a client’s instructions, the parties must record their respective roles and instructions in the applicable agreement. Do not assume that the same role applies to every workflow.

## Data minimization and backups

Collect and retain only information relevant to a stated account, service or engagement purpose. Do not ask for or keep unrelated information for possible future use. If irrelevant or obsolete information is identified, remove it unless a documented legal, contractual or security requirement requires it to be retained.

Numerimondes uses automated infrastructure backups where enabled and also makes separate remote backups that it manages. Backup copies must follow the same data-minimization and access rules as the source data. Their scope, destination and provider, storage region, access controls, retention period, rotation and deletion process must be documented. A backup copy may persist until its defined expiry or rotation; deletion from the live system alone does not mean every backup copy has already been erased.

## Use and access

We ask for information relevant to the agreed work and use it to deliver, support or account for that work. Before sensitive, personal or regulated information is shared, the parties must agree what is needed, who may access it, where it will be handled, and any required safeguards or instructions.

We do not use client information for unrelated purposes, publish it as a case study, or disclose it to another party without an agreed basis or authorization. Access should be limited to people who need the information for the engagement.

## Storage and lifecycle

The engagement terms should identify approved tools or hosting, client and provider responsibilities, transfer methods, retention or return expectations, and any deletion process. Where a project needs a specific location, residency, retention period or deletion evidence, that requirement must be agreed before the information is transferred.

We do not ask clients to send passwords, secret keys or other credentials through public contact forms or ordinary email. Use an agreed secure method for access information.

## Requests by clients and individuals

### Client organization data

An authorized client representative may request a copy, export or return of business data handled for the engagement, or request correction or deletion. Numerimondes verifies the representative’s authority and handles the request under the service agreement, documented client instructions and applicable legal, accounting, security and retention requirements. The agreement determines the request scope, format, timing and any limitations.

A company’s contractual request to export or return its business records is separate from the statutory rights of individuals whose personal data may appear in those records. Requests by individuals are assessed under applicable data protection law and the [account and CRM privacy notice](/legal/account-and-crm-privacy).

## Limits

Do not send information that is not needed for the work. Do not share information you are not authorized to provide. We do not claim a universal retention period, hosting location or certification across all projects; these depend on the systems and terms agreed for the engagement.
