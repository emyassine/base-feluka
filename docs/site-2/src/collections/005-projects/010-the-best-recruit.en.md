## What it is

The Best Recruit is Numerimondes’ remote recruitment service for employers. It centers on candidate qualification and relevant information for hiring decisions, without requiring the employer and recruitment team to be in the same location.

## How an engagement is scoped

- The role, skills, experience, availability and other relevant criteria are defined with the employer.
- Candidate qualification and the information shared with the employer are agreed for the assignment.
- The remote search, introductions, access to candidate information and any related services are set out in the scope.
- Candidate consent, fees, timing and each party’s responsibilities are confirmed before work begins.

## Discuss a hiring need

The Best Recruit is available for employer inquiries and scoped remote recruitment engagements. The services, candidate access, fees, timing and commitments available for a specific need are confirmed in writing before work begins. A recruitment engagement does not guarantee a hire or placement.

A fee rate of **46% of the candidate’s minimum monthly salary is planned**. Market research to validate the rate is planned soon. The final rate and service terms will be confirmed after the research.
