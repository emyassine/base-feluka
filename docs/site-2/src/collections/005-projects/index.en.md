# Our Projects

Numerimondes develops and operates services, platforms and technical standards. This collection explains what each project does, how it is used, and where its current scope begins and ends.

## Services

- [The Best Recruit](/projects/the-best-recruit) — remote recruitment built around candidate qualification and relevant information.
- [Numerimondes Cloud](/projects/numerimondes-cloud) — server setup, management, backups and operations for our own services and client environments.

## Platforms and standards

- [Feluqa](/projects/feluqa) — a proprietary development platform for building and operating connected business software.
- [SPMPST](/projects/spmpst) — the standard and toolchain used to compose and publish documentation sites.
