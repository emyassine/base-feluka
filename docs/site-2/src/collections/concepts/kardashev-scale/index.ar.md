# Kardashev Scale

A method of measuring a civilization’s level of technological advancement by the **amount of energy** it can harness and put to work — originally proposed by Nikolai Kardashev (1964).

| Type | Rough idea |
| --- | --- |
| I | Planetary energy budget |
| II | Full output of its star |
| III | Energy of its galaxy |

## Why it shows up in our research

When we talk about [[technological-acceleration|technological acceleration]] or “pushing technology as far as it can go”, the Kardashev ladder is a reminder: **the outer bound is physical and infrastructural**, not only software fashion.
