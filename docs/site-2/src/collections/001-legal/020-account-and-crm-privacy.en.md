# Account and CRM privacy notice

**Status: Draft.** This notice describes the account service at `numerimondes.com/account/`. The supplied data processing agreement covers one Cloud VPS 20 SSD subscription and names Contabo GmbH as processor and Contabo France SAS for data-centre operations. The agreement allows the server location to be selected in or outside the EU, or left to the provider. The selected region, whether this subscription hosts the CRM, other CRM providers, data flows, retention schedule and applicable CNDP formalities must be confirmed before this notice is approved as complete.

## Who is responsible

Numerimondes SARL AU, based in Casablanca, Morocco, publishes the account entry point. The entity operating the underlying CRM and the responsible party for each processing purpose must be confirmed before this notice is approved. This draft assumes Numerimondes determines purposes for its own account administration and security. For information a client asks Numerimondes to handle on the client’s behalf, the parties’ respective roles and instructions must be set out in their agreement.

The verified contact details for privacy requests must be added to the [publisher information notice](/legal/publisher-information). Until then, use the [contact page](/company/prologue/contact) and identify the request as a privacy request.

## Information the account service may handle

Depending on the account features used, the service may handle:

- registration and profile details, such as a person’s name, email address, organization and account status;
- information needed to authenticate an account and maintain a signed-in session;
- workspace membership, assigned roles and access changes;
- client project information, invoices, deliverables and support requests submitted through account features;
- security and audit records needed to investigate access issues and maintain accountability.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment card details or sensitive personal information through ordinary contact channels. Only provide information needed for the relevant account or engagement.

## Purposes

Account information is used to create and administer accounts, authenticate users, provide authorized access to the relevant workspace, support project and account workflows, respond to requests, protect the service and its records, and meet applicable legal or contractual responsibilities.

Numerimondes does not use account records for unrelated advertising or behavioral tracking under the [account access and workspace use policy](/policies/account-access) and [client and CRM data handling policy](/policies/data-handling).

## Access and recipients

Access should be limited to authorized Numerimondes personnel and collaborators who need it for their assigned work, and to the people the relevant client authorizes for its workspace. Under the supplied agreement, Contabo GmbH processes customer data for the covered VPS service, and Contabo France SAS is identified for data-centre operations. Whether that subscription hosts the CRM and which server region was selected must be confirmed. The agreement allows an outside-EU location or leaves the choice to the provider; the actual selection and any resulting international transfer safeguards must be confirmed before approval. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or under the applicable service agreement.

## Retention and account closure

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for its stated purpose and applicable contractual, security, accounting or legal obligations. No verified retention schedule or account deletion procedure is available for this draft. Numerimondes must confirm the periods or criteria for profile, workspace, project, authentication and audit information before approving this notice. Closing an account does not necessarily erase records that must be retained for a documented purpose.

## Requests and rights

You may contact Numerimondes to ask whether personal information about you is processed, request access or correction, or raise an objection where applicable. Identify the account and the information concerned, but do not send a password or secret token. The request route and identity-check process must be confirmed before this notice is finalized.

For information about rights under Moroccan Law 09-08, consult the [Moroccan CNDP’s guidance on data subject rights](https://www.cndp.ma/vos-droits-en-matiere-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/) and the [official law text](https://www.cndp.ma/images/lois/Loi-09-08-Fr.pdf). Applicable rights and procedures depend on the facts and the relevant law.

## Service boundary

The public site’s cookie and browser storage manager applies only to the site URL displayed in that manager. It is not a CRM account console and cannot inspect `HttpOnly` authentication cookies. See the [website cookies and browser storage policy](/policies/website-storage) for the public site’s local preferences.
