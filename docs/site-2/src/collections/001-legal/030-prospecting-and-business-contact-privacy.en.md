# Prospecting and business contact privacy

## Scope and responsible company

### What this notice covers

Numerimondes SARL AU may research organizations and professional opportunities for its own services and, under a defined arrangement, for a business partner. This notice describes information about organizations and identifiable people that may be encountered during that work. It also describes related contact-centre and recruitment activities when those activities are carried out.

This notice applies to research and contact carried out by Numerimondes and people acting under its instructions. A partner that independently decides why and how to use contact information is responsible for its own processing and must provide its own applicable information to people.

### Controller and contact

Numerimondes SARL AU is responsible for personal data it collects and uses to identify, assess and contact prospects for its own business. Its identity and contact details are in the [publisher information](/legal/publisher-information). Where Numerimondes works on a partner’s instructions, the parties’ roles and obligations depend on the written arrangement and the actual decisions each party makes.

## Information and sources

### Organization information

Research may cover organizations, their publicly presented activities, locations, services, public business channels and other information relevant to a defined business purpose. Information solely about a legal entity may fall outside personal-data rules, but information identifying an owner, employee, representative or other natural person is personal data.

### Professional contact information

Depending on the purpose, information may include a person’s name, professional role, publicly presented business email or telephone number, business profile, organization affiliation, and a record of the source, research date, contact history or response. Numerimondes does not seek sensitive personal details for ordinary prospecting.

### Public and platform sources

Research may use publicly viewable business listings and pages, including Google Maps and public websites, and professional or social platforms such as LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X and Reddit. It may also use information visible in WhatsApp groups or channels where the researcher is legitimately present and the group or channel rules permit the activity. The actual source and collection date should be recorded with the relevant contact record.

Information being visible online does not by itself authorize every collection, reuse, export, automated extraction or marketing message. Automated collection is used only where the source’s rules, access controls and applicable law permit it. If automated extraction is not permitted, a person may conduct limited manual research; manual collection still follows data-protection, confidentiality and platform rules.

## Purposes and partner work

### Numerimondes’ own business development

Numerimondes may use relevant business and professional contact information to identify organizations that could benefit from its services, prepare a relevant business approach, follow up on a request or introduction, maintain a suppression record after an objection, and manage the resulting business relationship.

### Research for a partner

A partner engagement must define its purpose, permitted sources and methods, data fields, access, retention, disclosure and outreach responsibility. Numerimondes shares identifiable contact information with a partner only where a valid legal basis, the written arrangement and applicable law permit it, and any required consent and information have been provided. A partner that chooses its own purpose or outreach is not automatically a processor acting only on Numerimondes’ instructions.

### Contact-centre services

Where Numerimondes provides contact-centre or calling services, the client, purpose, calling method, audience, scripts, contact lists, recording practice and applicable opt-out rules must be defined for that service. This notice does not mean every contact method or campaign is permitted in every country.

### Recruitment

Recruitment information is handled for a specific hiring or placement process, separately from commercial prospecting. Applicants are given information about the recruitment purpose, data use, recipients and retention. Candidate information is not added to a sales prospect list. Applicable CNDP formalities for recruitment and HR processing are assessed before the relevant processing begins.

## Outreach and choices

### Channel-specific requirements

Before contacting a person, Numerimondes assesses the law that applies to the person, country and communication channel. A public professional address or profile is not blanket consent to receive email, calls or messages. Morocco’s Law 09-08 restricts direct marketing through email, automated calls and similar means, subject to its terms and limited exception. Other countries may impose different consent, identification, do-not-contact or platform requirements.

### First contact and transparency

Where information was obtained from another source, Numerimondes identifies itself, explains the business purpose and relevant data use, and gives the person an accessible way to ask about the source or exercise applicable rights. Where required, this information is provided no later than the first communication. A person’s clear request not to be contacted for marketing is recorded and respected for Numerimondes’ relevant campaigns.

### No false identity or implied authorization

Researchers and callers must use an accurate name, role and business affiliation. They must not pose as an employee, customer, regulator, journalist, consultant or other person they are not, or invent a reason to obtain access or information. A research or intelligence label does not authorize misleading contact or conceal the actual purpose where disclosure is required. Whether the work is paid or unpaid does not change the need for a truthful role, a defined purpose and any required authorization.

## Research boundaries

### Manual research

Manual research is used to respect source restrictions where automated extraction is not allowed. It is not used to bypass a login, technical access control, rate limit, group rule, privacy setting or other restriction. Researchers do not collect credentials, private communications or information available only through unauthorized access.

### Security and vulnerability research

Passive review of lawfully accessible public information must remain within the agreed research scope. Scanning, probing, testing, attempting access, eliciting confidential information or assessing a facility’s physical or technical vulnerabilities requires explicit written authorization from the organization with authority over the relevant systems or site. A facility that has not requested or authorized an assessment is not treated as a client or test target. No researcher may imply that an assessment is commissioned when it is not.

### Data minimization

Collect only information relevant to the documented purpose. Do not collect sensitive personal information, private-life details, credentials or unrelated facts for ordinary business development. Verify important details before relying on them and correct or delete information that is inaccurate, irrelevant or no longer needed, subject to a documented legal or operational reason to retain it.

## Retention, recipients and security

### Retention

Contact records are kept only while needed for the documented prospecting, partner, recruitment or service purpose and applicable legal obligations. Each workflow sets a retention period or review criterion. A minimal suppression record may be retained to ensure a person who objected is not contacted again.

### Recipients and systems

Access is limited to Numerimondes personnel and authorized service providers who need it for the relevant task. Partner disclosure is governed by the section above and the applicable written agreement. CRM, hosting and international-transfer information is described in the [account and CRM privacy notice](/legal/account-and-crm-privacy) and [publisher information](/legal/publisher-information).

### Security

Contact lists and research notes are kept in access-controlled systems, shared only through approved channels, and not placed in public documents or unapproved personal accounts. Suspected loss or unauthorized disclosure is reported through the [security reporting policy](/policies/security-reporting).

## Rights and requests

### Requests by individuals

People may ask whether Numerimondes holds personal data about them, request access or correction, and exercise erasure, objection or other rights where applicable law provides them. The right to object to direct marketing is respected for the relevant purpose. Requests may also ask for the source of information and the organizations or categories of recipients to which it was disclosed, subject to applicable law.

### How to contact us

Use the [contact page](/company/contact) and identify the request as relating to prospecting, partner research or recruitment. Include the business contact details concerned and, where known, the organization and source. Numerimondes may ask for proportionate information to verify identity. Do not send passwords or unnecessary identity documents through ordinary contact channels.

### Legal information

For rights under Moroccan Law 09-08, see the [CNDP rights guide](https://www.cndp.ma/vos-droits-en-matiere-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/) and [official law text](https://www.cndp.ma/images/lois/Loi-09-08-Fr.pdf). Where the GDPR applies, information about indirectly collected data and its source is addressed in [Article 14](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/ojv#art_14), and the right to object to direct marketing is in [Article 21](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/ojv#art_21).
