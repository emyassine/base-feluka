These terms apply to website design, development, migration and related work supplied by **Numerimondes SARL AU** (“Numerimondes”) to the customer identified in a written proposal (“Client”). The proposal, these terms and any signed statement of work together form the agreement for the project.

They concern paid website development engagements. Ordinary access to the public Numerimondes website is governed by the separate [Website Terms of Use](/legal/website-terms-of-use). The commercial minimum and scope boundaries are described in the [Website Development Policy](/policies/website-development-policy).

## 1. Agreement and precedence

### 1.1 Formation

The agreement begins when the Client accepts a proposal in writing, signs the relevant order, or instructs Numerimondes to begin after receiving the proposal. The proposal identifies the project, deliverables, fees, schedule assumptions and any special terms.

### 1.2 Order of precedence

For a conflict, the following order applies: (1) a later signed amendment or statement of work, (2) the accepted proposal, (3) these terms, and (4) policies referenced for context. A proposal may vary these terms only when it identifies the provision being varied.

### 1.3 Changes

Any change to project scope, delivery dates, price or responsibilities must be recorded in writing. Numerimondes will explain the effect of a requested change on cost and timing before proceeding with the changed work.

## 2. Scope and project delivery

### 2.1 Defined deliverables

Numerimondes will perform only the work and provide only the deliverables listed in the accepted proposal. Features, integrations, content, language versions, hosting, maintenance and third-party services are included only when expressly listed.

### 2.2 Client inputs and cooperation

The Client will provide timely, accurate content, access, decisions, approvals and other materials reasonably required for the project. The Client is responsible for ensuring that it may lawfully provide these materials and for naming a person authorized to approve work.

### 2.3 Schedule dependencies

Dates are based on the assumptions in the proposal and the Client’s timely cooperation. Delays in receiving materials, decisions, access or payment may extend the schedule and may require a revised plan.

### 2.4 Review and acceptance

The proposal may define review stages, revision rounds and acceptance criteria. The Client must provide consolidated, specific feedback within the agreed review period. Work outside the included review rounds or criteria may require a written change request and additional fees.

## 3. Fees, taxes and payment

### 3.1 Project fees

Fees and payment milestones are stated in the proposal. Unless the proposal states otherwise, invoices are payable by the due date shown on the invoice. Taxes are treated as stated in the proposal or invoice and applicable law.

### 3.2 Third-party charges

Domain registration, hosting, paid software, licences, stock media, payment processing and other third-party charges are not included unless expressly listed. The proposal identifies who contracts with the provider and who pays each charge.

### 3.3 Late or disputed invoices

If the Client disputes an invoice, it should promptly identify the disputed amount and reason, and pay any undisputed portion on time. Numerimondes may pause affected work after written notice if an undisputed amount remains overdue, subject to applicable law and the proposal.

### 7.1 Client review before launch

The Client must review the website and supplied content before launch and report material issues against the agreed scope. The Client approves the final content and authorizes launch.

### 7.2 Launch dependencies

Handover items, training, source files, credentials and documentation are limited to those listed in the proposal. Credentials should be transferred through an agreed secure method. The Client must promptly replace temporary credentials and manage its own user access.

### 11.1 Suspension for cause

Numerimondes may pause work after notice if the Client materially fails to provide required inputs or payment, requests unlawful work, or creates a material security risk. Work will resume when the issue is resolved, and the schedule may be adjusted.

### 11.2 Termination

Either party may terminate for a material breach that remains uncured after written notice and a reasonable opportunity to cure. The proposal may also provide a termination-for-convenience process.

### 11.3 Work and payment on termination

On termination, the Client will pay for work properly completed and non-cancellable third-party commitments incurred up to the effective termination date, subject to the proposal and applicable law. Numerimondes will deliver paid-for work and return or delete Client materials as agreed, subject to legal retention requirements and any active backup cycle.

Except for express commitments in the agreement and rights that cannot be excluded by law, no additional warranty is given regarding business results, third-party services or uninterrupted, error-free operation.

Neither party is responsible for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, such as a widespread network outage, natural event, governmental restriction or failure of a critical third-party service, provided it takes reasonable steps to reduce the effect and resumes performance when practicable.
