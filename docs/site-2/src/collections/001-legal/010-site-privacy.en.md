# Privacy and Site Data

This draft covers the public Numerimondes website. The account and CRM area has a separate [account and CRM privacy notice](/legal/account-and-crm-privacy). Publisher details and the production hosting arrangement are recorded in the [publisher information notice](/legal/publisher-information).

## Contact form

The contact form is processed in your browser. When you choose **Continue to WhatsApp**, the site opens a WhatsApp conversation draft containing the name and message you entered, plus any optional organization and email fields. Review the draft before sending it. WhatsApp and its operator process messages under their own terms and privacy information; this site does not receive the form fields through its own server.

You can contact Numerimondes without using the form by choosing another contact method published on the site.

## Search, cookies and site storage

Site search uses a local index delivered with the site. Search terms are processed in your browser. The site does not configure an analytics or advertising tracker.

The **Manage cookies** control applies only to the site URL displayed in its dialog. Its necessary first-party consent cookie records your optional-storage choices for up to 180 days. Theme selection, currency selection, and the marketplace shortlist and reviews are necessary to the features you use and remain available without optional consent. They are not used for tracking and appear as checked, disabled choices. Optional storage covers downloaded orientation catalogues and other optional browser storage. The manager lists and can remove storage it detects for this site, including cookies readable to page scripts, local and session storage, IndexedDB databases, caches and service workers. Language selection follows the page URL and is not stored as a language preference. Browser scripts cannot inspect other websites' storage or read `HttpOnly` cookies. Read the [website cookies and browser storage policy](/policies/website-storage) for the category details and manager limits.

When you reject or revoke optional storage, the site removes related entries it can identify in this browser. Necessary theme, currency and marketplace data remain available. The [cookie and browser storage guide](/articles/manage-cookies) explains how to review and change these choices.

## External resources

Some pages load images hosted by Unsplash and link to external services such as WhatsApp and LinkedIn. When your browser requests content from an external provider, that provider may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details. Review the provider's own privacy information for its practices.

## Hosting and security logs

The website uses Contabo Cloud VPS infrastructure. For one Cloud VPS 20 SSD subscription, the data processing agreement names Contabo GmbH as processor and Contabo France SAS for data-centre operations. Hosting infrastructure may process technical request information, including IP addresses, requested paths, timestamps and browser details, for delivery, security and troubleshooting. The server location selected for this subscription, the VPS serving this site, and the applicable log and backup retention must be confirmed from the deployed configuration before this draft is approved. See the [publisher and website information](/legal/publisher-information) for provider details.
