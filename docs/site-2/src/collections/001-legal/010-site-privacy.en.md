# Privacy and Site Data

This draft describes the Numerimondes website. It does not cover separate applications or services operated by Numerimondes or third parties.

## Contact form

The contact form is processed in your browser. When you choose **Continue to WhatsApp**, the site opens a WhatsApp conversation draft containing the name and message you entered, plus any optional organization and email fields. Review the draft before sending it. WhatsApp and its operator process messages under their own terms and privacy information; this site does not receive the form fields through its own server.

You can contact Numerimondes without using the form by choosing another contact method published on the site.

## Search and site preferences

Site search uses a local index delivered with the site. Search terms are processed in your browser. The site does not configure an analytics or advertising tracker. Your browser may store interface preferences such as the selected color theme.

## External resources

Some pages load images hosted by Unsplash and link to external services such as WhatsApp and LinkedIn. When your browser requests content from an external provider, that provider may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details. Review the provider's own privacy information for its practices.

## Hosting and security logs

The hosting environment may process technical request information, including IP addresses, requested paths, timestamps and browser details, for delivery, security and troubleshooting. Log access and retention depend on the hosting provider and deployment configuration.

## Questions or corrections

For questions about this notice, use the [contact page](/contact/). This draft should be reviewed against the final hosting configuration and company privacy process before being treated as a complete legal notice.
