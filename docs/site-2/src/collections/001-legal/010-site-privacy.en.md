# Privacy and Site Data

<img src="/images/legal/site-privacy-water-reflections.webp" alt="Soft light breaks into narrow reflections across a dark, gently rippled water surface.">

This legal notice is maintained under the [Policy Management Policy](/policies/policy-management), which prevails on governance of published legal documents. This page is limited to privacy and data handling on the public website.

## Scope and publisher

### Public website

This notice covers the public Numerimondes website. Account-area processing is described in the [account and CRM privacy notice](/legal/account-and-crm-privacy). Publisher details and hosting information are in the [publisher information notice](/legal/publisher-information).

## Contact information

### Contact form processing

The contact form is processed in your browser. The site does not receive the form fields through its own server.

#### WhatsApp handoff

When you choose **Continue to WhatsApp**, the site opens a conversation draft containing the name and message you entered, plus any optional organization and email fields. Review the draft before sending it. WhatsApp and its operator process messages under their own terms and privacy information.

### Other contact routes

You can contact Numerimondes without using the form by choosing another contact method published on the site.

## Search and browser storage

### Search

Site search uses a local index delivered with the site. Search terms are processed in your browser. The site does not configure an analytics or advertising tracker.

### Necessary storage

The **Manage cookies** control applies only to the site URL displayed in its dialog. Necessary storage supports the features you use and remains available without optional consent. These items are not used for tracking and appear as checked, disabled choices.

#### Consent choice

A necessary first-party consent cookie records your optional-storage choices for up to 180 days.

#### Site preferences and marketplace

Theme and currency selections, along with marketplace shortlists and reviews, are treated as necessary feature storage.

### Optional storage and changes

Optional storage includes downloaded orientation catalogues, the PWA cache of visited public pages and files, and other optional browser storage. The service worker is installed only when you allow offline storage. It skips account and API routes and does not cache responses marked private or no-store. When you reject or revoke offline storage, the site unregisters its worker and removes its PWA cache, along with other related entries it can identify. Necessary theme, currency and marketplace data remain available.

#### Review your choices

The [cookie and browser storage guide](/articles/manage-cookies) explains how to review and change these choices.

### Detection limits

The manager lists and can remove storage it detects for this site, including cookies readable to page scripts, local and session storage, IndexedDB databases, caches and service workers.

#### Browser boundaries

Language selection follows the page URL and is not stored as a language preference. Browser scripts cannot inspect other websites’ storage or read `HttpOnly` cookies.

#### Policy details

See the [website cookies and browser storage policy](/policies/website-storage) for storage categories and manager limits.

## External resources

### Images hosted by external providers

Some pages load images hosted by Unsplash. When your browser requests an image, the provider may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details.

### Links to external services

The site links to services such as WhatsApp and LinkedIn. If you follow a link, the provider processes your visit under its own terms and privacy information.

## Hosting, delivery and security logs

### Numerimondes Cloud management

Numerimondes Cloud manages the website infrastructure. The applicable hosting provider and server region are listed in the [publisher and website information](/legal/publisher-information); they can vary by deployment.

### Content delivery locations

#### Webserver request information

Hosting infrastructure may process technical request information for delivery, security and troubleshooting.

##### Information in server logs

This may include IP addresses, requested paths, timestamps and browser details. The deployment-specific server region is stated in the [publisher information notice](/legal/publisher-information).

#### CDN

Where a CDN is used, its edge locations may be in other regions and may change as the delivery configuration changes. See the [publisher and website information](/legal/publisher-information) for hosting providers and infrastructure processors.

### Backups and retention

#### Automated and manual backups

Automated backups are enabled for the current webserver, and Numerimondes also makes separate manual remote backups.

#### Retention and location records

The CDN provider and edge locations, remote backup destination and region, and applicable log and backup retention and deletion arrangements depend on the active deployment configuration. Contact Numerimondes for details applicable to a particular service.

## Personal data requests

### Rights under Moroccan law

People whose personal data is processed may use the rights available under Morocco’s Law 09-08.

#### Information, access and correction

These include information and access rights, and rights to request correction or updating.

#### Erasure, blocking and objection

Erasure or blocking may be requested where statutory conditions are met. Objection rights apply in the circumstances set by the law, including a free objection to direct marketing.

#### CNDP references

See the [CNDP guide to data subject rights](https://www.cndp.ma/vos-droits-en-matiere-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/) and [Law 09-08](https://www.cndp.ma/images/lois/Loi-09-08-Fr.pdf) for applicable conditions and procedures.

### Rights where GDPR applies

Where the GDPR applies, people may have rights to rectification, erasure, restriction and objection, subject to its conditions and exceptions.

#### Data portability

Portability is limited to personal data the person provided, where processing is automated and based on consent or contract. It is not a general right to export every record held about a company. See [GDPR Article 20](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679).

### How to make a request

Use the [contact page](/company/contact) and identify your message as a personal data request. State the relevant website interaction and the right you want to exercise.

#### Identity checks and safe contact

Numerimondes may ask for information proportionate to confirming your identity and authority. Do not send passwords, access tokens or unnecessary identity documents through ordinary contact channels.
