# Privacy and Site Data

This notice covers the public Numerimondes website. The account and CRM area has a separate [account and CRM privacy notice](/legal/account-and-crm-privacy). Publisher details and the production hosting arrangement are recorded in the [publisher information notice](/legal/publisher-information).

## Scope and publisher

### Public website

This notice covers the public website. Account-area processing is described in the [account and CRM privacy notice](/legal/account-and-crm-privacy); publisher and hosting details are in the [publisher information notice](/legal/publisher-information).

## Contact information

### Contact form and WhatsApp

The contact form is processed in your browser. When you choose **Continue to WhatsApp**, the site opens a WhatsApp conversation draft containing the name and message you entered, plus any optional organization and email fields. Review the draft before sending it. WhatsApp and its operator process messages under their own terms and privacy information; this site does not receive the form fields through its own server.

### Other contact routes

You can contact Numerimondes without using the form by choosing another contact method published on the site.

## Search and browser storage

### Search

Site search uses a local index delivered with the site. Search terms are processed in your browser. The site does not configure an analytics or advertising tracker.

### Necessary storage

The **Manage cookies** control applies only to the site URL displayed in its dialog. Its necessary first-party consent cookie records your optional-storage choices for up to 180 days. Theme selection, currency selection, and the marketplace shortlist and reviews are necessary to the features you use and remain available without optional consent. They are not used for tracking and appear as checked, disabled choices.

### Optional storage and changes

Optional storage covers downloaded orientation catalogues and other optional browser storage. When you reject or revoke optional storage, the site removes related entries it can identify in this browser. Necessary theme, currency and marketplace data remain available. The [cookie and browser storage guide](/articles/manage-cookies) explains how to review and change these choices.

### Detection limits

The manager lists and can remove storage it detects for this site, including cookies readable to page scripts, local and session storage, IndexedDB databases, caches and service workers. Language selection follows the page URL and is not stored as a language preference. Browser scripts cannot inspect other websites' storage or read `HttpOnly` cookies. See the [website cookies and browser storage policy](/policies/website-storage) for the category details and manager limits.

## External resources

### Images and linked services

Some pages load images hosted by Unsplash and link to external services such as WhatsApp and LinkedIn. When your browser requests content from an external provider, that provider may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details. Review the provider's own privacy information for its practices.

## Hosting, delivery and security logs

### Numerimondes Cloud management

Numerimondes Cloud manages the website infrastructure. The applicable hosting provider and server region are listed in the [publisher and website information](/legal/publisher-information); they can vary by deployment.

### Content delivery locations

#### Webserver

Hosting infrastructure may process technical request information, including IP addresses, requested paths, timestamps and browser details, for delivery, security and troubleshooting. The deployment-specific server region is stated in the [publisher information notice](/legal/publisher-information).

#### CDN

Where a CDN is used, its edge locations may be in other regions and may change as the delivery configuration changes. See the [publisher and website information](/legal/publisher-information) for hosting providers and infrastructure processors.

### Backups and retention

#### Automated and manual backups

Automated backups are enabled for the current webserver, and Numerimondes also makes separate manual remote backups.

#### Retention and location records

The CDN provider and edge locations, remote backup destination and region, and applicable log and backup retention and deletion arrangements depend on the active deployment configuration. Contact Numerimondes for details applicable to a particular service.

## Personal data requests

### Rights under Moroccan law

People whose personal data is processed may use the rights available under applicable law. Under Morocco’s Law 09-08, these include information and access rights, and rights to request correction, updating, erasure or blocking where the statutory conditions are met. Objection rights also apply in the circumstances set by the law, including a free objection to direct marketing. The [CNDP guide to data subject rights](https://www.cndp.ma/vos-droits-en-matiere-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/) and [Law 09-08](https://www.cndp.ma/images/lois/Loi-09-08-Fr.pdf) explain the applicable conditions and procedures.

### Rights where GDPR applies

Where the GDPR applies, people may have rights to rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, subject to the conditions and exceptions in that regulation. Portability is limited to personal data the person provided, where processing is automated and based on consent or contract; it is not a general right to export every record held about a company. See [GDPR Article 20](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679).

### How to make a request

Use the [contact page](/company/prologue/contact) and identify your message as a personal data request. State the relevant website interaction and the right you want to exercise. Numerimondes may ask for information proportionate to confirming your identity and authority; do not send passwords, access tokens or unnecessary identity documents through ordinary contact channels.
