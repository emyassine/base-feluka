# About Numerimondes

## Ideas, strategy and the ability to act.

Numerimondes SARL AU is an independent consulting and solutions company established in Casablanca, Morocco. We investigate opportunities, support business decisions and develop practical projects across business operations, technology and related fields.

Our activities draw on a wide corporate purpose encompassing management and business development consulting, information systems, digital transformation, digital marketing, security, training, contact-centre operations, recruitment and IT-related equipment and solutions. Our active services and developing initiatives are presented separately throughout this website.

## Founder

### Yassine El Moumen

**Founder & Managing Director**

Yassine's background spans contact-centre operations, project management, training, consulting and software engineering. Experience with operational teams and business projects informs his approach to practical problem-solving and the needs of decision-makers.

Research and continued learning are a central part of his work, from business models and markets to systems architecture and emerging technologies. This exploration feeds the company's consulting, innovation and project development activities.

## Delivery model {#delivery-model}

### Built around the mission.

Numerimondes is founder-led and operates through **project-based assignments**. A single accountable contact oversees qualification, proposal direction and delivery coordination. Depending on the needs, we bring together independent developers, consultants, operators and specialist companies for the mission.

This arrangement allows each project to be defined by the work required. Responsibilities, reporting, confidentiality and deliverables are established contractually; Numerimondes remains accountable to its clients for the assignments it contracts to deliver.

## Our working principles

**Clarity** — Make business goals, constraints and decisions understandable.

**Curiosity** — Research opportunities and explore thoughtful alternatives.

**Practicality** — Design around the operating realities of people and organizations.

**Accountability** — Agree responsibility, scope and commercial conditions before execution.

**Control** — Consider ownership, maintainability and operational autonomy in the proposed solution.
