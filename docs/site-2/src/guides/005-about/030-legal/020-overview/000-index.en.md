## Registered Purpose (*Objet Social*)

The text below is taken verbatim from the company's statutes (*statuts de la société*). It is the legal purpose registered with Moroccan authorities — not a marketing summary.

Day-to-day positioning and services are described under [Company](/about/company) and [Services](/services).

**Patente:** management consulting.

The Company has for its purpose, in Morocco and abroad, for its own account as well as for the account of third parties:

- Commercial development consulting;
- Information systems consulting and software development;
- Information technology consulting and digital transformation;
- Digital marketing consulting;
- Consulting, control, and audit in security, safety, and cybersecurity;
- E-learning and training; organisation of turnkey seminars, conferences, and modular training programmes; in-company training and pedagogical engineering;
- Management and operation of telephone contact centres;
- Recruitment, human-resources strategy consulting, career development, and skills evaluation;
- Representation and exploitation of all patents, trademarks, or licences for IT and industrial products, accessories, and solutions;
- Purchase, import, sale, and export of all IT equipment and materials; management, installation, fit-out, and operation of any commercial or industrial unit suited to the activities listed above.

And more generally, all commercial, industrial, financial, movable, and real-estate operations related directly or indirectly to the above purposes, or likely to favour their achievement and development.
