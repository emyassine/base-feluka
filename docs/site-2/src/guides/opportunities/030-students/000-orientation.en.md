# Opportunities for students

## A useful next step, without a fee

Students and recent graduates can contact Numerimondes for **free initial orientation**. We help you clarify your interests, identify skills to build, and choose practical next steps toward study, projects or work.

Try the [online student orientation tool](/opportunities/students/online-orientation) to turn your studies, interests and strengths into a few practical paths to explore.

### Free orientation can include

- A short conversation about your goals, studies and constraints.
- Guidance on career directions and skills relevant to your interests.
- Feedback on your CV, portfolio or project idea.
- Signposting to suitable learning resources, events and opportunities.
- Selected group sessions on professional basics, digital tools and entrepreneurship.

There is no fee for this initial orientation. It does not guarantee a job, internship, admission, funding or placement. If a later activity has a cost, we explain it before you decide; you can decline without affecting access to free guidance.

## For students who want to contribute

We may share suitable internships, project briefs, workshops or introductions when an opportunity is available and the fit is clear. Participation depends on the partner's criteria and a written description of the role, expectations and any compensation.
