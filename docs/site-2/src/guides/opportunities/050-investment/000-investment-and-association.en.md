# Investment and business association

Are you looking to invest in a business, find an operating partner, or join forces around a promising project? Numerimondes can help start a structured conversation with people whose goals and capabilities may fit.

## Who this is for

- Investors exploring opportunities in Morocco or cross-border projects.
- Founders looking for a co-founder, strategic partner or experienced operator.
- Professionals interested in joining or building a venture together.
- Business owners considering a joint offer, local representation or shared delivery.

## Start with a clear profile

Tell us about your experience, sector interests, geography, contribution, investment range (if applicable), preferred role and time horizon. We can discuss fit, introduce relevant parties with consent, and agree what information is appropriate to share.

Any association is subject to independent due diligence, agreed responsibilities and written terms. A conversation or introduction is not an investment offer, a promise of return, or a commitment to match or transact. Financial and legal advice should come from appropriately qualified professionals.
