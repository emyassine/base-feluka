# Morocco partnerships

## Accounting and advisory firms

Local knowledge of clients' operating realities can reveal needs that deserve technical or organizational support: manual reconciliations, disconnected systems, missing visibility, or tools that no longer fit the business.

Numerimondes can assess the issue, present options, and coordinate a scoped mission. Your firm keeps its client advisory relationship and professional remit. This partnership path is for advisers operating in Morocco, where local context and professional rules can be handled directly.

### How it works

1. Identify a potential need and ask the client whether they welcome an introduction.
2. With the client's permission, hold an initial qualification discussion.
3. Prepare a proposal when there is a suitable assignment.
4. Numerimondes takes contractual responsibility for the work it undertakes.
5. Agree any eligible referral fee or commission in writing and in accordance with applicable professional rules.

No client information is requested without appropriate authorization. Participation does not require an obligation to refer.
