# Worldwide opportunities

We collaborate with independent professionals and organizations that <span class="silky-i">identify opportunities</span>, contribute specialist expertise, or open access to new markets.

Numerimondes is headquartered in Casablanca and brings together operational management, project delivery, consulting, and software engineering. We consider international work when the client context, delivery team, and local requirements make it practical.

Introduce qualified opportunities, connect prospective clients with Numerimondes, or join project teams when your expertise fits an assignment. This may include software engineering, Rust, infrastructure, business consulting, security, analysis, documentation, training, design, and other relevant specialties.

International collaboration is assessed per assignment. It depends on client needs, applicable legal and tax requirements, data handling, language, delivery capacity, and a clear written agreement. Referral or commission terms are agreed in writing for eligible assignments.
