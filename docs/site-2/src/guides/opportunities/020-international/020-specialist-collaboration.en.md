# Specialist collaboration

## Specialist work

We assemble project teams according to the mission. Opportunities may involve Rust, PHP, software engineering, infrastructure, security, analysis, documentation, training, design, or other specialist work.

## Engagement terms

Each collaboration is defined by deliverables, availability, confidentiality, quality expectations, intellectual-property terms, and payment milestones. Cross-border work also depends on applicable legal, tax, data-handling, language, and client requirements. An introduction does not promise immediate work.

## Working principles

- Keep client relationships and consent at the center of each introduction.
- Define responsibilities and scope before making commitments.
- Formalize commercial terms in writing.
- Build complementary teams for credible, longer-term client outcomes.

## Start a conversation

Tell us where you operate, the sectors or clients you know, what you contribute, and the kind of collaboration you have in mind. We will assess fit and agree a practical next discussion.
