# International opportunities

We work with independent professionals and complementary businesses when a project, client context, and local requirements make cross-border collaboration practical. Each opportunity is assessed individually; an introduction does not promise immediate work.

## Business development and referrals

Introduce qualified prospects, help identify projects, or support commercial development on agreed service lines. Terms cover lead attribution, eligibility, scope, payment conditions, and conflicts of interest. Commissions are not assumed until agreed in writing and may depend on collected revenue.

## Commercial and delivery partners

Companies with complementary services can explore joint proposals, subcontracting, or coordinated delivery. Agree each party's role, client responsibilities, and commercial terms for the specific opportunity.

## Specialist work

We assemble project teams around each mission. Opportunities may involve Rust, PHP, software engineering, infrastructure, security, analysis, documentation, training, design, or other specialist work.

## Engagement terms

Agree deliverables, availability, confidentiality, quality expectations, intellectual-property terms, and payment milestones before work begins. Cross-border work also depends on applicable legal, tax, data-handling, language, and client requirements.

## Working principles

- Keep client relationships and consent at the center of each introduction.
- Define responsibilities and scope before making commitments.
- Formalize commercial terms in writing.
- Build complementary teams for credible, long-term client outcomes.

## Start a conversation

Tell us where you operate, the sectors or clients you know, what you contribute, and the kind of collaboration you have in mind. We will assess fit and agree a practical next discussion.
