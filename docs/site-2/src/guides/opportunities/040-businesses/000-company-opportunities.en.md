# Opportunities for companies

Numerimondes helps organizations turn skills needs and early-career opportunities into clear, well-scoped next steps. Companies can propose internships, entry-level roles, student projects, mentoring sessions or practical workshops.

## Work with students

- Share an internship or junior role with its responsibilities, requirements, location and compensation.
- Propose a supervised project brief that gives students a real business problem to explore.
- Offer a company visit, mentoring session or practical workshop.
- Discuss recruitment support when you need help defining a role or reaching suitable candidates.

We review each opportunity before sharing it. We agree the selection process, student support, data handling and any fees in writing. A listing does not guarantee applications or a hire.

## Business launch and market research packs

Professionals planning to open an independent practice can request a scoped market study and launch plan. This can be useful for a **medical practice or clinic**, a **law firm**, or an **accounting and advisory practice**.
