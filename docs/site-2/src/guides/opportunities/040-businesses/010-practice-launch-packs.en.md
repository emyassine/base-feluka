# Practice launch packs

Opening a professional practice is a major investment. A focused market study can help you test the opportunity, compare locations and plan the first stage before committing to premises, equipment or staffing.

## Choose a practice type

### Medical practice or clinic

Assess the local catchment, population and likely demand; map nearby providers and service gaps; compare candidate locations; and outline service mix, patient access and launch priorities.

### Law firm

Map the local legal-services landscape, client segments and competing offers; identify a focused practice area; compare locations and channels; and prepare a positioning and client-development plan.

### Accounting or advisory practice

Assess the density and needs of local businesses, existing firms and underserved segments; compare service packages and pricing signals; and plan outreach, capacity and a staged launch.

## What a pack can include

- A defined study area, target clients and research questions.
- Desk research, competitor and offer mapping, and available demand indicators.
- Interviews or other primary research when agreed and feasible.
- Location or service-area comparison, with assumptions made explicit.
- A launch plan, key risks, decision points and recommended next steps.

We scope the research depth, sources, timeline and price with you before starting. Findings support your decision; they are not a guarantee of commercial success. Market research does not replace professional legal, accounting, medical, licensing or regulatory advice, approvals or inspections.
