## If production is genuinely needed

Some files need something that doesn't exist yet — a 3D volume study, a CAD drawing, a render. When that happens, we scope exactly what's needed and bring in the right contractor for it. Whether that contractor is hired by us or directly by you is a detail, not a principle — what matters is that you know precisely what Numerimondes is delivering versus what's subcontracted. We keep our own footprint on this as light as it can be; the story and the assembly are ours, the physical production is whoever does it best for that specific need.

## Who this is for

- **Tender candidates** who already have technical material but need it turned into a file that actually satisfies the cahier des charges — often paired with [Deal Structuring](/services/tender-support/deal-structuring).
- **Companies raising capital or credit** who need a pitch deck or bank-facing dossier built from existing data, not from a blank page.
- **Architects, engineering firms, or industrial suppliers** who already hold the tender and the technical depth, and want the narrative and assembly handled separately from their own production work.
