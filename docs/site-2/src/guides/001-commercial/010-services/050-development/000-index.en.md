## Pricing method

Development is bespoke by nature, so we do not pretend to fix a price before scoping. What we commit to instead:

- **An order of magnitude within the first conversation** — before any contract, so you know roughly what you are walking into.
- **A closed quote after scoping**, broken into milestones tied to shippable increments, not calendar time.
- **Milestone payment**, never 100% upfront and never a single invoice at the end.

| Formula | Scope | Typical basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Scoping sprint** | Requirements, architecture direction, estimate | Fixed price, 1–2 weeks |
| **Build phase** | Design → build → handover, per milestone | Fixed price per milestone, agreed after scoping |
| **Ongoing evolution** | Post-launch features, capped monthly capacity | Monthly retainer, order of magnitude given upfront |
