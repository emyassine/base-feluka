## Why this matters

We manage our own production infrastructure on the same stack we offer clients — the same hardening, monitoring, and backup discipline described on the [Features page](/services/cloud/features). If a security update or a configuration change is worth applying to a client server, we apply it to our own platforms too, since downtime or a breach on our own products costs us directly.

We are not publishing specific uptime percentages or response-time figures on this page as a marketing claim — real performance depends on the tier, the workload, and the hardware involved, and any figure we haven't independently verified for a given period is not one we will assert here. Clients under an active management agreement receive their own service reporting as part of that agreement.
