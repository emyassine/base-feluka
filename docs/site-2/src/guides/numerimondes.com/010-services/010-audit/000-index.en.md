## The idea

A company's value does not live only in its tools. An audit that only lists software is useful — and incomplete. **Pseudo-consulting stops at the stack.** Real benefit appears when we look at the full value-creation chain: processes, information flows between teams, repetitive work — not only the apps that support them.

You choose the depth. We never force Level 2.

## What you receive

### Deliverable A — Audit report

PDF · **10–15 pages** for Level 1 · extended for Level 2

- Mapping of current tools (Level 1) and, for Level 2, the full value chain
- Break points identified: vendor dependency and lock-in risk (Level 1); organisational bottlenecks and low-value manual work (Level 2)
- Current state vs target state, with automation and AI opportunities ranked by function
- **Total cost of ownership (TCO)** of the current stack, projected over **three years**

### Deliverable B — Restitution conference

**1h30–2h**, on-site, in front of decision-makers and managers — guided walkthrough of the report, sector benchmark, live demonstration of [Webkernel](/numerimondes/docs/oss/webkernel) in a self-hosted environment, and a priced proposal for next steps.

### Deliverable C — Prioritised roadmap

**1–3 pages maximum.** A simple matrix: quick wins · structural projects · medium-term bets.

## Indicative packages

| Formula | Scope | Timeline | Price |
