## We don't sell advice — we sell relevance and results

We deliberately push back on the word "consultant" when it implies someone who only talks. A consultant who only gives advice is a cost centre. We show up having already done the thing we are advising on — and we leave you with something that changed, not a deck.

That framing is not ours alone. It is how Julie Sweet, CEO of Accenture, describes the two things clients actually pay for:

## Pricing

We prefer to name a price. When a sprint is genuinely bespoke, we give an order of magnitude before any contract is signed — never an open-ended day rate with no ceiling.

| Formula | Scope | Timeline | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Focused sprint** | Single decision or workstream (e.g. go-to-market plan, IS strategy direction) | 4 weeks | On request — order of magnitude given upfront |
| **Full sprint** | Multi-workstream transformation plan, board-ready | 6–8 weeks | On request — order of magnitude given upfront |
| **Advisory retainer** | Ongoing founder-level advisory, capped hours per month | Monthly | On request |

Payment follows the same discipline as the rest of our services: milestone-based, never 100% upfront, never invoice-at-the-end.
