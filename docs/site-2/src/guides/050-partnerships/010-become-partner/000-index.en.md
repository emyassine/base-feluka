# Join the Numerimondes partner framework

Numerimondes works with independent professionals who can introduce organizations, understand their needs, and help deliver useful services. The framework combines shared tools, training, introductions, and clear written terms for each commercial engagement.

## Three ways to work together

- **Deliver your own services.** You contract with your own clients and retain the fees you invoice.
- **Introduce or sell Numerimondes services.** Suitable opportunities are recorded and attributed before work begins; any commission is agreed in writing for the relevant offer and paying entity.
- **Work with the wider partner network.** With the parties’ agreement, a partner may join a specific project or deliver a defined service.

## What Numerimondes provides

Partners can receive onboarding on the offers and sales process, proposal and communication templates, a website and CRM setup plan, and introductions to relevant network members. The exact tools and delivery commitments are confirmed for each partner and project.

## How the framework starts

1. Discuss your experience, market, and the services you want to offer.
2. Agree the scope, territory, data handling, and deal attribution in writing.
3. Set up the independent business and the tools required for the work.
4. Start with a defined opportunity and review the collaboration together.

The framework is non-exclusive. Partners remain independent, set their own working methods, and are responsible for their own local registration, taxes, and professional obligations. No commission rate, revenue, client volume, or employment relationship is implied by this overview. Commercial terms are agreed separately before a deal starts.
