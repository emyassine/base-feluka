# Our fees: how we are paid

Numerimondes is paid for agreed recruitment, consulting, software development and operational services. The price depends on the work required to reach the agreed outcome, the delivery risks and the expertise involved.

We explain the scope, deliverables, price or pricing basis, payment schedule and each party’s responsibilities in writing before an engagement begins. A capability describes an area we can explore; a service offer describes work that can be scoped and purchased. The [capabilities library](/capabilities/) and [service catalogue](/services/marketplace) serve different purposes.

::: info Catalogue figures are examples
The service catalogue currently identifies itself as a demonstration. Its partners, reviews, starting prices and payment terms are illustrative sample data, not approved public rates or a promise of current availability. Catalogue examples are shown in MAD, excluding VAT (HT). The amount, scope, taxes, schedule and availability for any real engagement must be confirmed in a written proposal.
:::

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Start with the value to be created</span>

We begin with the business outcome, the current situation and the decisions the work should support. Where the information allows, the proposal identifies a baseline and practical measures of success. It then sets out the work, investment, assumptions and risks needed to pursue the outcome. An estimate of potential value helps a client compare options; it is not a guarantee of financial or operational results.

| Decision step | What we clarify |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Define the need** | The business problem, people affected and current process or system. |
| **Describe the outcome** | The desired change and how progress or acceptance will be assessed. |
| **Compare approaches** | Existing products, a process change, a focused discovery or custom delivery. |
| **Agree the investment** | Scope, price, dependencies, risks, schedule and responsibilities. |

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Choose a pricing model that fits the work</span>

A fixed price works when scope and inputs are clear. When an important input changes, a formula or review schedule can make the effect explicit. More complex pricing needs better data, clear approval rights and a defined process for updating an offer.

| Pricing approach | Fits when | What to define |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fixed price** | The deliverables, assumptions and acceptance conditions are stable enough to agree in advance. | Included work, exclusions, change requests and payment milestones. |
| **Formula-based price** | A small number of measurable inputs drive the price. | The inputs, source of each input, formula, floor or cap, and when it applies. The candidate-salary formula shown above is demo data, not an approved live rule. |
| **Periodically reviewed price** | Costs, capacity or market conditions can change during an ongoing service. | Review dates, evidence, notice, approval and the effect on the existing commitment. |
| **Scoped quotation** | Several interdependent or uncertain factors affect effort, risk or value. | Discovery needed, assumptions, decision points and a written approval before the scope changes. |

Before changing a price model, clarify the commercial objective, customer need, relevant market inputs, available data and who can approve a change. Revisit the model when those conditions materially change. Numerimondes does not represent the demo catalogue as a live dynamic-pricing system or promise automatic real-time price changes.

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>How a fee is structured</span>

| Engagement model | How the fee is set | When it can fit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fixed-scope project** | An agreed price for defined deliverables, assumptions and acceptance criteria. | Requirements are clear enough to define and accept the work. |
| **Discovery or feasibility study** | A separate fee for the assessment and its agreed outputs. Any credit against later implementation must be stated in the proposal. | The next decision depends on research, requirements, options or feasibility evidence. |
| **Milestone-based delivery** | Scope, payment and acceptance points are agreed for each approved phase. | The work is larger or should proceed through decision points. |
| **Time-based specialist engagement** | A defined amount of consulting, research or engineering capacity for an agreed period, with limits and reporting documented. | The work needs specialist capacity while its detailed tasks evolve. |
| **Recurring operations and support** | A recurring fee for agreed infrastructure, monitoring, maintenance, improvement or advisory responsibilities. | Support continues after an initial project or setup. |

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>What changes the quotation</span>

| We assess | Examples of what affects the scope |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Outcome and acceptance** | The result sought and how the client will determine that the work is complete. |
| **Current environment** | Existing systems, workflows, data quality and integrations. |
| **People and expertise** | Stakeholders, specialist skills, equipment and licensing required. |
| **Risk and operating needs** | Security, availability, privacy, compliance and recovery considerations. |
| **Delivery conditions** | Location, access, documentation, support and handover. |
| **Dependencies and timing** | External providers, uncertainty and the requested timeline. |

## <span class='silky-bbi+115'>Payment and proposal terms</span>

The demonstration catalogue currently shows **40% on approval, with the balance at milestones or delivery**, and monthly billing for recurring services. These are sample terms from the demo data, not universal or confirmed payment terms.

| The written proposal should state | What it makes clear |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Scope and deliverables** | What is included, what is excluded and how acceptance works. |
| **Price and payment** | The amount, currency, applicable taxes, due dates and payment milestones. |
| **Ownership and confidentiality** | Ownership and licensing, confidentiality and any permitted use of client material. |
| **Responsibilities and change** | Client inputs, dependencies, how scope changes are approved and their effect on price or timing. |
| **After delivery** | Handover, documentation, support period and any ongoing service. |

We may recommend an existing product, a limited process change or staged implementation where that better serves the business.
