# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2 November 2026**

Numerimondes SARL AU, based in Casablanca, Morocco, is responsible for information it receives through this website and the contact channels listed here. We handle personal information for the purpose of responding to enquiries, discussing potential work, and managing related business communications.

## Information you choose to share

The contact form on this site runs in your browser. When you submit it, your browser opens WhatsApp with a draft message addressed to Numerimondes. The website does not send the form fields to a Numerimondes server or store them. Review the draft before choosing whether to send it.

If you contact us through WhatsApp, email, or telephone, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, organization, and message. WhatsApp and email are provided by third parties and handle information under their own terms and privacy practices.

## Website operations

The site uses local search. It does not submit search terms to a Numerimondes search service. The hosting provider may process standard technical request data, such as IP address, requested page, time, and browser information, in server logs for delivery, reliability, and security. The site may store a display preference in your browser, such as your selected color theme.

## Use and retention

We use enquiry information to respond, assess and follow up on a requested business discussion, and maintain necessary business records. Access is limited to people who need it for these purposes. We retain communications only for as long as needed for the enquiry and related business or legal obligations, then delete or securely dispose of them when no longer needed.

We do not sell personal information. We do not ask you to submit sensitive personal information through this site or WhatsApp contact form.

## Your choices and requests

You may ask us about personal information we hold, request correction, or object to or ask us to stop using information where applicable. Contact [contact@numerimondes.com](mailto:contact@numerimondes.com) with the subject “Privacy request”. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Personal data processing in Morocco is governed by applicable law, including Law No. 09-08. You can find information about the law and the rights it provides on the [Moroccan CNDP website](https://www.cndp.ma/textes-et-lois/) and its [rights guidance](https://www.cndp.ma/personnes-concernees/).
