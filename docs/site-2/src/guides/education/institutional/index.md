# Institutional

Numerimondes works with universities and schools under an **academic accompaniment** framework: filmed conferences and complementary lectures at no direct cost to the establishment, with student registration and verifiable attestations on this site.

## For students

1. Browse [upcoming events](/community/events/upcoming)
2. [Create an account](https://www.numerimondes.com/app/register) or [log in](https://www.numerimondes.com/app/login)
3. Register for the session, attend, and receive an online attestation

## For establishments

Partner schools get a dedicated operational contact, event pages for SEO, attendance reports, and content for their own communication — under the written convention.
