# Opportunities

## Create opportunities together.

Numerimondes works with independent businesses, professional advisers, commercial partners and specialists who help identify opportunities and deliver useful solutions. Collaboration is organized around clear responsibilities, client consent and written commercial arrangements.

**Explore opportunities** → `/company/contact?topic=partnership`

## Accounting & advisory firms

Your knowledge of clients' operating realities can reveal problems that deserve dedicated technical or organizational support: manual reconciliations, disconnected systems, missing visibility or tools that no longer suit the business.

Numerimondes can assess the issue, present options and coordinate a scoped mission. Your firm retains its client advisory relationship and professional remit.

**How it can work:**

1. You identify a potential need and ask the client whether they welcome an introduction.
2. With permission, we hold an initial qualification discussion.
3. We prepare a proposal where there is a relevant assignment.
4. Numerimondes takes contractual responsibility for work it undertakes.
5. Any eligible referral fee or commission is handled under a specific written agreement and applicable professional rules.

No client information is requested without appropriate authorization. Participation does not require an obligation to refer.

## Independent business developers & referral partners

Introduce qualified prospects, help identify projects and support commercial development on agreed service lines. We can collaborate with independent consultants, agents and businesses with a relevant network.

Terms cover lead attribution, eligibility, scope, payment conditions and conflicts of interest. Commissions are not assumed until explicitly agreed in writing; calculations may depend on collected revenue.

## Freelancers & specialist companies

We assemble project teams according to the mission. Opportunities may involve Rust, PHP, software engineering, infrastructure, security, analysis, documentation, training, design or other specialist work.

Collaborations are defined by deliverables, availability, confidentiality, quality expectations, intellectual-property terms and payment milestones. An introduction does not promise immediate work.

## Commercial & delivery partners

Companies with complementary services can examine joint proposals, subcontracting or coordinated service delivery. Each entity's role and commercial terms are agreed per opportunity.

## Why work with Numerimondes?

- A defined entry point for opportunity assessment and project coordination.
- Access to complementary expertise organized for each mission.
- A shared interest in credible, longer-term client relationships.
- Scope and commercial terms formalized before commitments.
- A wide field of potential projects across consulting, technology and operations.

## Start a conversation

Tell us where you operate, the sectors or clients you know, what you contribute, and the kind of collaboration you have in mind. We will assess fit and agree a practical next discussion.

**Contact us about a partnership** → `/company/contact?topic=partnership`
