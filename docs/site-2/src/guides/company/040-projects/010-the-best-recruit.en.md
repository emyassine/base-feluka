## What it is

The Best Recruit is the recruitment firm of Numerimondes. Its approach centers on candidate qualification and on giving employers clear, relevant information to decide.

## How an engagement is scoped

- The role, skills, experience, availability and other relevant criteria are defined with the employer.
- Candidate qualification and the information shared with the employer are agreed for the assignment.
- The search, introductions, access to candidate information and any related services are set out in the scope.
- Candidate consent, fees, timing and each party’s responsibilities are confirmed before work begins.

## Discuss a hiring need

The Best Recruit is available for employer inquiries and scoped recruitment engagements. The services, candidate access, fees, timing and commitments available for a specific need are confirmed in writing before work begins. A recruitment engagement does not guarantee a hire or placement.
