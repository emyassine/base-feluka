## What it is

The Best Recruit is the recruitment firm of Numerimondes. Its approach centers on candidate qualification and on giving employers clear, relevant information to decide.

## How it is designed to work

- Candidates organized around skills, experience, availability and relevant preferences.
- An initial qualification to help employers assess fit.
- Search and filtering on appropriate, relevant criteria.
- Clear rules for introductions, contact access, candidate consent and fees.

## Current status

The firm is being prepared. Employer onboarding, candidate participation, pricing and service commitments will be communicated when processes are ready. No placement, access to candidate records or response time is promised today.
