## A focused team, led by the founder

Numerimondes works through project-based assignments. The founder leads qualification, proposal and delivery, and stays accountable to the client throughout. A small core brings complementary technical skills, while independent experts and partner organizations join when the work requires them.

The team is the group of people who deliver a given assignment together. This describes how work is organized, not anyone’s employment status. Decisions stay close to the work, with clear ownership and direct communication.

## Principles that guide the work

- **Clarity:** Goals, constraints and decisions are stated plainly.
- **Curiosity:** We research the problem and consider alternatives before recommending.
- **Practicality:** Solutions fit how people and organizations operate.
- **Accountability:** Responsibility, scope and commercial conditions are agreed before execution.
- **Control:** Ownership, maintainability and operational autonomy are part of each solution.

## Agreed before work begins

Each assignment defines its scope and deliverables, each party’s responsibilities, reporting and communication, confidentiality, and commercial conditions. Numerimondes remains accountable for the work it contracts to deliver, including contributions from specialists and partners.

## Join the network

We welcome developers, consultants, operators and specialist firms with strong expertise who take responsibility for their work. Scope, responsibilities and commercial terms are agreed for each collaboration before it starts.
