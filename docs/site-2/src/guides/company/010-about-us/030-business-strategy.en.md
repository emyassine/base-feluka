## Why a single flagship

A focused offer gives clients a clear reason to choose us and gives the company a sustainable base. Our other projects, Feluqa and SPMPST, serve that base: they are how we build software and websites quickly and with control.

## Current status

This is the intended direction. The Best Recruit is being prepared for launch and its services are not yet open.
