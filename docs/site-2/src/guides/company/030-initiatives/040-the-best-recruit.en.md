# The Best Recruit

**Coming soon · Preparing for launch**

## Better information. More meaningful introductions.

The Best Recruit is a recruitment and talent initiative in preparation at Numerimondes. Its planned approach centers on structured candidate qualification and clear, relevant information for employers making recruitment decisions.

### What we are working toward

- A candidate pool organized around skills, experience, availability and relevant preferences.
- An initial qualification process designed to help employers assess fit.
- Employer search and filtering based on appropriate, relevant criteria.
- Clear rules for introductions, contact access, candidate consent and commercial arrangements.

These describe the **planned service direction**, not a live placement service or an existing searchable candidate platform.

## Status

The service is being prepared. Employer onboarding, candidate participation, pricing, privacy information and service commitments will be communicated when the platform and processes are ready.

## Expressions of interest

Organizations and potential collaborators can contact Numerimondes to discuss future needs. No placement, access to candidate records or response timeline is promised at this stage.
