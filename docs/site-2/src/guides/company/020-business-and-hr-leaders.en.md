## Business operations, systems and software

We can help when:

- Operations depend on a few people or manual work that does not scale.
- Systems do not fit the way the business runs or do not work well together.
- A new service or product needs validation before a larger investment.
- You want to own and control your technology.
- A custom application or integration needs to be built and maintained.

For a first conversation, bring the problem and what is at stake, the result you want, your timeframe and constraints, and who makes the decision. We will clarify whether we can help and what a first step could look like. Any assessment or quotation is confirmed separately, in writing.

## Recruitment for HR and hiring teams

The Best Recruit is our remote recruitment service for employers and HR teams, wherever they are based. Before work begins, we agree on the role, search scope, candidate information, remote interactions, fees, timing and responsibilities for each mandate. A recruitment engagement does not guarantee a hire or placement.

Tell us which roles and seniority levels you are hiring for, the location and expected volume, your timeline, and any confidentiality requirements. Availability, response timing and placement commitments are confirmed for each remote mandate.
