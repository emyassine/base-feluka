## Founder-led today

Numerimondes is currently founder-led and works through **project-based assignments**. One accountable contact oversees initial qualification, proposal direction and delivery coordination.

## Assemble expertise around each assignment

Depending on the work, we may collaborate with independent developers, consultants, operators and specialist companies. The team and scope are shaped by the needs of the assignment.

## Agree on responsibilities

Responsibilities, reporting, confidentiality and deliverables are agreed before work begins. Numerimondes remains accountable to clients for the assignments it contracts to deliver.
