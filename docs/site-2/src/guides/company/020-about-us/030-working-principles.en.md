# Working Principles

**Clarity** — Make business goals, constraints and decisions understandable.

**Curiosity** — Research opportunities and explore thoughtful alternatives.

**Practicality** — Design around the operating realities of people and organizations.

**Accountability** — Agree responsibility, scope and commercial conditions before execution.

**Control** — Consider ownership, maintainability and operational autonomy in the proposed solution.

## How we aim to work

We aim to build a small, technically strong team with a flat structure and a high level of individual responsibility. Keeping the team focused should help us make decisions close to the work and deliver at a steady pace. We want to create the conditions for a trusted network of partners and independent specialists to contribute where their expertise is most useful.

Our business strategy is to focus on a flagship offering that can sustain the company, then develop complementary services and initiatives around it. The Best Recruit is one initiative we are preparing in this direction. Its current status and planned scope are described on [its initiative page](/company/initiatives/the-best-recruit).
