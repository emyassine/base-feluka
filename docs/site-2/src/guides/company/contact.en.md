## What you can contact us about

- A consulting, systems or software project
- A remote hiring need for an employer (The Best Recruit)
- A partnership or specialist collaboration
- A technical discussion on Feluqa or SPMPST

## What to include

Your organization, the problem and what is at stake, the result you want, your location and timeframe, and how you prefer to be contacted.

We review your message, clarify the scope of the discussion and propose the next step. Any assessment, quotation or partnership arrangement is confirmed separately.
