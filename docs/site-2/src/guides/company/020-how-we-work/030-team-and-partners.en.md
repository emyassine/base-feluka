## What “team” means here

The team is the group of people who deliver a given assignment together: the founder, independent specialists and partner organizations. The term describes how work is organized, not anyone’s employment status.

## Join the network

We want to meet developers, consultants, operators and specialist firms with strong expertise who take responsibility for their work. Scope, responsibilities and commercial terms are agreed up front, for each collaboration.
