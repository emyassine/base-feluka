## Founder-led, project-based

Numerimondes works through project-based assignments. The founder leads qualification, proposal and delivery, and stays accountable to the client throughout.

## Agreed before work begins

- Scope and deliverables
- Responsibilities of each party
- Reporting and communication
- Confidentiality
- Commercial conditions

Numerimondes remains accountable to the client for what it contracts to deliver, including the work of specialists and partners it brings in.
