## Recruitment: The Best Recruit

The Best Recruit is our remote recruitment service for employers and HR teams, wherever they are based. We agree the role, search scope, candidate information, remote interactions, fees, timing and responsibilities for each mandate before work begins. A recruitment engagement does not guarantee a hire or placement.

## Operations and systems around hiring

Process, tooling and workforce-operations questions can be handled as consulting or systems missions, independently of recruitment.

## What to tell us

- The roles and seniority you hire for
- Location and expected volume
- Your timeline
- Any confidentiality requirement

Availability, response timing and placement commitments are confirmed for each remote mandate.
