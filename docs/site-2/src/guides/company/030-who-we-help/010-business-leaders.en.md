## Situations where we can help

- Operations depend on a few people or on manual work that does not scale.
- Systems do not fit the way the business actually runs, or do not talk to each other.
- A new service or product needs to be validated before heavy investment.
- You need to own and control your technology instead of depending on it.
- A custom application or integration has to be built and maintained properly.

## What to bring to the first conversation

- The problem and what is at stake
- The result you want
- Your timeframe and constraints
- Who makes the decision

## What you get from it

A clear view of whether we can help and what a first step would look like. An assessment or quotation is confirmed separately, in writing.
