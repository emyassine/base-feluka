## VPS, bare metal & bring your own — quoted

These three tiers are priced individually because the right number depends on real variables: how much CPU and RAM the workload needs, whether the hardware is dedicated or shared, where it needs to be physically located, and — for bring-your-own — what state the existing hardware is already in. We would rather quote accurately than publish a number that doesn't hold once we see your actual requirements.

For **Bring Your Own**, the arrangement is specifically:

1. **Share your server specs** — hardware details and current OS state. We assess and provide a fixed management quote.
2. **Agree a flat management fee** — covering configuration, monitoring, and ongoing management, with no per-task billing and no surprise charges for a security incident or a backup restore.
3. **We apply the management layer** — OS hardening, stack configuration, monitoring, and backups, without taking ownership of the hardware.
4. **You stay in control** — the server remains yours; you retain root access at all times. We add management, we do not take ownership.

## Payment terms

Whichever tier applies, invoicing is direct from Numerimondes SARL AU — you are not billed by a third-party hosting provider. Shared hosting is billed annually as shown above. VPS, bare metal, and bring-your-own management fees are billed monthly unless otherwise agreed in the quote.
