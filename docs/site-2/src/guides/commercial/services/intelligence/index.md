## Pricing method

Whether you are an independent consultant or an industrial operator, we do not bill per-token fees or place ongoing rent on your own knowledge assets. What we commit to:

- **An initial scoping exchange** — identifying operational bottlenecks, hardware requirements, and data structure before any contract.
- **A closed-scope quotation**, broken into delivery milestones (knowledge setup → pipeline integration → enablement).
- **Absolute ownership** — you retain all data indices, local model configurations, training systems, and workflows.

| Formula | Scope | Typical basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Scoping sprint** | Knowledge-indexing plan, hardware sizing, signal mapping | Fixed price, ~1 week |
| **System build & integration** | Local model deployment, RAG setup, signal-pipeline integration | Fixed price per milestone |
| **Enablement & seminars** | On-site or remote training, team onboarding, operational documentation | Fixed price per programme |

Payment stays milestone-based, as on every Numerimondes service.
