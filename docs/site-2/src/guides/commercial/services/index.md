## Why small is the offer, not the excuse

Most consulting firms scale by adding layers between you and the person who understands your problem. We scale by adding proof. Every mission we take, someone with a name and a track record is accountable for it end to end — usually the founder himself.

This is not a romantic story about boutiques. It is a pricing and delivery decision: fewer people, less overhead, closed scopes, real deliverables, prices you can put in front of a board.

An audit does not commit you to a build. A build does not commit you to hosting with us. Each service stands on its own; together, they are how a company goes from "we should probably modernise" to running systems it actually owns.

## How we price

We would rather quote a number than dress up a day rate. On every service you will find one of:

- **A closed package price**, when the scope is standard enough to fix in advance (Audit, most of Automation).
- **An order of magnitude and a milestone structure**, when the work is bespoke (Development, Infrastructure, strategic Consulting) — so you know what you are walking into before a contract exists.

Payment is always milestone-based. No 100%-upfront, no invoice-at-the-end surprises.

Each offer page is presentation-first: scope, includes/excludes, pricing method, CTA. Optional depth (methodology, pricing model, cloud detail) is one level under the offer — no deeper tree.
