# Roadmap and acceptance

[Target] Delivery is gated by evidence. Estimates are planning ranges, not dates
or promises. The complete [English enterprise plan](../roadmap/02.enterprise-delivery.en.md)
retains F0–F12; the [French plan](../roadmap/02.enterprise-delivery.fr.md) retains
its detailed task history. Their F8/F9 meanings conflict; retain source-qualified
IDs and all evidence until an editorial crosswalk is prepared. The controlling
architecture, product and compilation direction remains as stated in the three
owner-designated chapters.

## Implementation sequence

1. Establish a compiling representative product that exercises the target
   System Control Panel; document current failures and the UI feature/MSRV boundary.
2. Finish shared view/editor conformance and the accessible component foundations
   (F0–F3), then product panels/data workflows (F4/F8/F10).
3. Complete declarative scanner/API/runtime wiring (F11/F12), preserving server
   authorization and deterministic output.
4. Deliver Live reconciliation/shared replay (F5), standalone scaffolds and
   deployment/restore (F6), and installable lifecycle (F7) through their gates.
5. Advance local and workstation delivery (F9) with a bookstore workflow. The
   local-host product requirement is selected; test it without Internet. Resolve
   runtime, storage, device identity and conflict policies through the spike.

Dependencies are not permission to omit operations, security or recovery gates.
The detailed plans retain their original evidence and scope.
