# Build with Feluqa

Creating an **Application** with Feluqa's developer tools (the software
development kit, or SDK) is free of charge for personal projects and
commercial work. Individuals and organisations can build Applications with
it; contractors and subcontractors can build them for clients when authorised
to do so. You are free to use the **Products** you create for yourself, your
organisation, or your client. This right to use your Products is guaranteed.

## Feluqa Platform and licensing

The **Feluqa SDK** is the development API and tools used to create Applications.
Its use for Application creation is free of charge. The shared runtime and
administration services (the **Feluqa Platform**) provide capabilities such
as identity, access control, audit, and configuration; the Platform is
distinct from a customer's Product.

Both the SDK and Platform are proprietary software owned by NUMERIMONDES SARL
AU. Proprietary describes ownership; it does not take away your guaranteed
right to create Applications and use the Products built with them, or your
right to contribute to Feluqa and help others build with it through the
collaboration process.

The applicable terms distinguish rights to Feluqa's SDK and Platform
components from rights in a Product, its Applications and Modules, and
customer-specific source code. These terms use Feluqa's
[canonical taxonomy](../010-get-started/000-introduction/030-taxonomy.en.md).

Feluqa currently has one SDK. Additional SDKs may be developed for different
audiences or application profiles.

Some Feluqa software components (Rust crates) will be released under
open-source licences over time. Each release will identify the components,
licence, and applicable versions. Other components may remain unpublished to
meet the security, operational,
contractual, or strategic needs of civilian, research, military, and
government deployments. This can apply even when a component contains no
client-specific business logic or deployment configuration. These decisions
are made component by component; keeping source private is not, by itself, a
security guarantee.

Employees, subcontractors, and AI agents may work within the rights granted to
their organisation. Third-party components remain subject to their own
licences and notices.

For full copyright and licence information, consult the licence materials
distributed with the relevant source code or the Licences resource in the
System Control Panel, when available.

## Why Feluqa is proprietary

Proprietary software is not inherently good or bad; it is an ownership and
distribution model. For Feluqa, it lets NUMERIMONDES steward an integrated
platform and sustain work across SDKs, runtimes, development tools, deployment,
and operations. Application creation remains free, and organisations can
build with their own engineers. The product strategy is to provide systemic
breadth in the platform while letting users and their agents provide domain
depth through their own Modules.

The commercial ambition is to build a durable, differentiated position in
military software and software for small and medium-sized enterprises.
Proprietary technology is part of that strategy: the goal is to create a
distinct product that is difficult to substitute and sustain it as a business.
This is an ambition, not a claim that Feluqa has already achieved a tenfold
technical advantage or market leadership.

We believe that lasting companies build differentiated technology and serve
markets where they can establish a defensible position. Feluqa's own thesis
adds that modern development tools make broad platform capabilities more
attainable for a small team, while users and their agents can shape the
specialized business workflows. We describe this as [“breadth is now
affordable, depth belongs to users”](../010-get-started/030-agentic-development/010-agent-ready-extensible-modules.en.md).

Proprietary ownership does not by itself prove that software is safer, more
reliable, or technically superior. Those qualities require implementation,
evidence, and review. The ownership model describes who controls Feluqa and
what rights are granted; it does not replace security, operational, or
contractual commitments.

## Contributions

We welcome contributions and want to work closely with the people who make
them. Contact Feluqa through [GitHub](https://github.com/feluqa/) to discuss
your idea. Maintainers will work with you on scope, access, and contribution
terms before work is incorporated. See the
[Contribution Guide](030-contribution-guide.en.md) for how to get started and
[Core Contribution](../../feluqa-internals/index.en.md) for engineering
guidance. The Feluqa Platform remains proprietary; agreed terms make clear how
your contribution may be used in proprietary or open-source components.

## Want a Product Built with Feluqa?

NUMERIMONDES works with organisations that want a Product created with Feluqa.
Start a conversation about the users, workflows, operating environment, and
delivery goals. A written proposal will define the scope, deliverables, rights
in customer-specific work, Feluqa usage rights, deployment, support,
maintenance, and any fees before work begins. Commercial terms may include
development, licensing, support, or maintenance fees; terms are agreed for
each engagement.

### Build with your own team

Organisations can have their own engineers build and maintain Products with
Feluqa. NUMERIMONDES can discuss the required access, usage rights, onboarding,
and support. A client's engineers may propose improvements to the Feluqa SDK;
changes are reviewed against the development guidelines, product architecture,
and security requirements, and contribution terms are agreed before accepted
work is incorporated.

### Find people to build with Feluqa

NUMERIMONDES can use its in-house recruitment activity to help identify and
assemble a qualified team for a Feluqa project. A directory of people with
Feluqa experience is planned; profiles will be published only with each
person's agreement and with their experience clearly described.
