# Licence and Ownership

Feluqa is proprietary software owned by NUMERIMONDES SARL AU. All rights are
reserved unless NUMERIMONDES grants specific rights in a separate written
licence. Access to the repository does not, by itself, grant permission to
use, copy, modify, publish, distribute, sublicense, or create derivative
works from Feluqa. The repository's [`LICENSE`](../../../../LICENSE) file states
the operative terms. Third-party components remain under their own licences
and notices.

## Why Feluqa is proprietary

Feluqa is being developed as a long-term platform business, not as an open
source project. The product strategy is to provide systemic breadth in the
platform while letting users and their agents provide domain depth through
their own modules. Keeping the platform proprietary gives NUMERIMONDES control
of its integrated roadmap and the ability to fund and protect sustained work
across the SDK, runtimes, development tools, deployment, and operations.

The commercial ambition is to build durable, defensible leadership in military
software and software for small and medium-sized enterprises. Proprietary
technology is part of that strategy: the goal is to create a distinct product
that is difficult to substitute, then sustain it as a business. This is an
ambition, not a claim that Feluqa has already achieved a tenfold technical
advantage, a monopoly, or market leadership.

This position draws on the strategic argument in Peter Thiel's *Zero to One*
that lasting companies build differentiated technology and serve markets
where they can establish a defensible position. Feluqa's own thesis adds that
modern development tools make broad platform capabilities more attainable
for a small team, while users and their agents can shape the specialized
business workflows. The product intent document describes this as
["breadth is now affordable, depth belongs to users"](../../../../README_Product_Document_Intent.en.md#2-strategic-thesis-breadth-is-now-affordable-depth-belongs-to-users).

Proprietary ownership does not by itself prove that software is safer, more
reliable, or technically superior. Those qualities require implementation,
evidence, and review. The ownership model describes who controls Feluqa and
what rights are granted; it does not replace security, operational, or
contractual commitments.

## Contributions

Feluqa is not accepting contributions under an open source contribution
licence. A public repository or review discussion does not grant a right to
submit code for incorporation. External contributions may be considered only
under written terms agreed with NUMERIMONDES before submission; those terms
must make clear the rights granted for the contribution and its use in
Feluqa.

If contributor terms have not been provided and agreed, do not submit code,
documentation, designs, or other material with the expectation that it will
be incorporated into Feluqa. Use the
[Contribution Guide](03-contribution-guide.en.md) for the repository workflow,
and contact NUMERIMONDES to discuss contribution terms first.
