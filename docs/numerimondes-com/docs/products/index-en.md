WebKernelPHP is a modular backend architecture designed for organizations that need to build, operate, and sustain digital systems over the long term.

It is not a framework built for rapid prototyping or short-lived applications.
It is a kernel-oriented approach to structuring backend systems that must remain stable, governable, and evolvable over years.

WebKernelPHP is part of the Numerimondes ecosystem and reflects its core principles:

- software as infrastructure
- governance before tooling
- modularity over fragmentation
- durability over speed
- autonomy over dependency

---

## Why WebKernelPHP exists

Most backend stacks today are optimized for:

- speed of delivery
- trend-driven architectures
- short product cycles
- external platform integration

Over time, this leads to:

- architectural debt
- fragmented systems
- dependency on vendors and services
- loss of internal engineering clarity

WebKernelPHP exists to provide a different foundation.

It is designed for organizations that need:

- control over their systems
- structured backend architecture
- long-term maintainability
- predictable evolution of software
- internal engineering consistency

---

## What it is

WebKernelPHP is a backend kernel designed to support:

- modular application structures
- multi-context environments (organizations, tenants, domains)
- long-lived operational systems
- internal governance of software
- extensible modules and capabilities

It acts as a stable core around which applications, modules, and services can evolve without destabilizing the entire system.

---

## What it is not

WebKernelPHP is not:

- a starter kit
- a CRUD framework
- a SaaS builder
- a microservice-first stack
- a trend-driven developer tool

It is intended for serious systems that must operate reliably and coherently over time.

---

## Core principles

### Kernel-first architecture

A central, stable core that governs:

- users
- permissions
- modules
- system capabilities
- lifecycle

### Modularity

Applications are structured as modules that integrate into a governed system, not isolated products.

### Long-term lifecycle

Systems built with WebKernelPHP are expected to:

- evolve over years
- survive team changes
- support institutional continuity

### Governance

Technology decisions are embedded in the architecture itself, enabling:

- clarity
- control
- accountability

---

## Who it is for

WebKernelPHP is designed for:

- institutions
- organizations operating critical systems
- engineering teams building internal platforms
- long-term software initiatives

It is particularly relevant when systems must:

- be owned internally
- operate in constrained or unstable environments
- avoid dependency on fragile external ecosystems
- remain maintainable over long periods

---

## Position within Numerimondes

WebKernelPHP is not a standalone product.

It is a core component of the broader Numerimondes approach to:

- sovereign software
- durable system design

It translates research, doctrine, and operating principles into a practical architecture.

WebKernelPHP will progressively expand through:
